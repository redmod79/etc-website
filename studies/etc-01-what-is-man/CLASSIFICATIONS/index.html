    Evidence Classifications: What Is Man?¶

    Tool-verified classification audit trail for etc7-01-what-is-man. Each positional evidence item is presented with its claim, supporting evidence, and the full Decision Tree walkthrough. Hebrew/Greek parsing verified by running hebrew_parser.py and greek_parser.py against the actual text.

    E-Item Classifications (Tree 3)¶

    E12 — Thoughts perish at death¶

    Claim: "When breath (ruach) departs, in that very day his thoughts perish" Reference: Psalm 146:4 Evidence: The psalmist teaches: do not trust in princes or the son of man, for when his breath departs and he returns to the earth, "in that very day his thoughts perish."

    Classification Walkthrough:

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 — אָבְדוּ (אבד, "perish") applied to human thoughts at death. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): Subject is עֶשְׁתֹּנֹתָיו ("his thoughts") belonging to a literal human whose רוח departs — antecedent (v.3) is בן־אדם ("son of man"). PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): אָבְדוּ — lemma אבד, Qal Perfect 3cp. Intransitive: "to perish, cease to exist." Subject עֶשְׁתֹּנֹתָיו (fp noun + 3ms suffix). No ambiguity. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Psalm 146 is wisdom/hymnic psalm with direct didactic instruction (vv.3-4 teach not to trust in mortal princes). PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E13 (no remembrance), E14 (silence), E15 (dead know nothing), E16 (no knowledge in sheol), E70 (land of forgetfulness). PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond.

    E13 — No remembrance in death¶

    Claim: "In death there is no remembrance of God; in the grave none gives thanks" Reference: Psalm 6:5 Evidence: David appeals to God for deliverance on the basis that the dead cannot remember or praise him.

    Classification Walkthrough:

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 — "death" (מות) and "no remembrance" describe cessation of conscious activity. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): David speaking of human beings in death generally; implicit subject of "gives thanks" is any person in Sheol. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): אֵין (negative particle of non-existence) + בַּמָּוֶת (prep ב + noun מות, "in death") + זִכְרֶךָ (noun זכר + 2ms suffix). יוֹדֶה — ידה Hiphil.Impf.3ms ("gives thanks") negated by rhetorical מִי ("who?"). PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Psalm — individual lament, direct first-person speech stating theological premise. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E12, E14-E16, E70. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond.

    E14 — The dead go to silence¶

    Claim: "The dead praise not the LORD; they go down into silence" Reference: Psalm 115:17 Evidence: A declarative statement contrasting the living (who praise the LORD) with the dead (who go to silence).

    Classification Walkthrough:

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 — "dead" (מות, Qal participle) and "silence" (דומה) — cessation vocabulary. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): הַמֵּתִים (Qal.Ptcp.mp of מות) — literal dead humans as a class. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): מֵּתִים (מות, Qal.Ptcp.mp.Abs) substantive = "the dead." יְהַלְלוּ (הלל, Piel.Impf.3mp) negated by לֹא = "they do not praise." יֹרְדֵי (ירד, Qal.Ptcp.mp.Cst) = "ones going down to." דוּמָה (Noun.fs.Abs) = "silence." PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Hymnic psalm with declarative theological statement. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E12, E13, E15, E16, E70. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond.

    E15 — The dead know nothing¶

    Claim: "The dead know not any thing; their love, hatred, and envy have perished" Reference: Ecclesiastes 9:5-6 Evidence: Qoheleth states that the dead have no knowledge, no share in anything under the sun, and their emotions have perished.

    Classification Walkthrough:

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 — "perished" (אָבְדָה from אבד, Qal Perf.) applied to the dead's faculties. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): הַמֵּתִים (מות, Qal.Ptcp.mp) — literal dead humans. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): אֵינָם יֹודְעִים (אין + ידע Qal.Ptcp.mp) = "they do not know"; מְאוּמָה = "anything" — total negation. אָבְדָה (אבד, Qal.Perf.3fs) = "has perished" applied to love/hatred/jealousy. חֵלֶק אֵין לָהֶם עוֹד לְעוֹלָם = "no share for them again forever." PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Wisdom literature, direct didactic assertion. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E12, E13, E14, E16, E70. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond.

    E16 — No knowledge in sheol¶

    Claim: "In sheol there is no work, nor device, nor knowledge, nor wisdom" Reference: Ecclesiastes 9:10 Evidence: Qoheleth commands diligent work because there is absolutely no activity or cognition in sheol.

    Classification Walkthrough:

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 — אֵין ("there is not") + four nouns of activity/cognition applied to all in sheol. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): Implied "you" (אַתָּה, 2ms) — generic literal human. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): אֵין (negation particle) governs מַעֲשֶׂה (deed), חֶשְׁבֹּון (account), דַעַת (knowledge), חָכְמָה (wisdom). Locative בְּשְׁאֹול = "in sheol." Total absence stated. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Wisdom literature, direct didactic exhortation. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E12-E15, E70. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond.

    E20 — The soul that sins shall die¶

    Claim: "The soul (nephesh) that sins shall die" Reference: Ezekiel 18:4, 20 Evidence: God declares as a universal legal principle that the sinning nephesh itself dies.

    Classification Walkthrough:

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 — "die" (תָמוּת, lemma מות) applied to the sinning nephesh. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): הַנֶּפֶשׁ הַחֹטֵאת — "the soul that sins." Universal legal principle, any literal human person. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): תָמוּת — מות, Qal Imperfect 3fs. Legislative force: "shall die." חֹטֵאת — חטא, Qal Participle fs, attributive. No ambiguity. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Direct prophetic speech — God speaking in first person. Didactic. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E36, E49, E12-E16. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond.

    E36 — God can destroy soul and body¶

    Claim: "God can destroy (apollymi) both soul (psyche) and body (soma) in gehenna" Reference: Matthew 10:28 Evidence: Jesus teaches his disciples to fear God who is able to destroy the entire person — both soul and body — in gehenna.

    Classification Walkthrough:

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 — "destroy" (ἀπολέσαι, lemma ἀπόλλυμι G622) applied to soul and body. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): τὸν δυνάμενον (God) destroys human ψυχή and σῶμα. Literal human beings are the target. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): ἀπολέσαι — ἀπόλλυμι G622, Aorist Active Infinitive. Active voice: God performs the action. Aorist: complete event. Complements δυνάμενον ("the one being able to destroy"). PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Direct didactic speech of Jesus to the Twelve. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E20, E49, E50. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond.

    E40 — David has not ascended to heaven¶

    Claim: "David is not ascended into the heavens" Reference: Acts 2:34 Evidence: Peter states as historical fact in his Pentecost sermon that David — dead and buried, with his tomb still present — has not gone to heaven.

    Classification Walkthrough:

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 — "not ascended" — cessation/absence vocabulary applied to a dead patriarch. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): David (Δαυίδ, proper noun) — literal historical person. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): ἀνέβη (ἀναβαίνω G305, Aorist Active Indicative 3S) negated by οὐ (G3756) — simple factual denial in indicative mood. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Apostolic sermon (Peter's Pentecost address) — direct didactic speech. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E41 (Heb 11:39-40), E38 (Dan 12:2), E12 (Psa 146:4). PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond.

    E49 — Wages of sin is death; gift of God is eternal life¶

    Claim: "The wages of sin is death; the gift of God is eternal life" Reference: Romans 6:23 Evidence: Paul presents death and eternal life as an exhaustive binary — wages vs. gift, with no third option.

    Classification Walkthrough:

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 — θάνατος ("death") as wages of sin; ζωή αἰώνιος as God's gift (contrast implies life is not inherent). Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): Wages applied to sinners generally; gift to those in Christ. Literal humans. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): θάνατος (G2288, Nom Sg M) — predicate nominative in equative clause. ζωή αἰώνιος (G2222 + G166, Nom Sg F) — parallel equative. δέ (G1161) marks adversative contrast: death vs. life. Binary, unqualified. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Pauline epistle, didactic theological argument. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E20, E36, E50. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond.

    E50 — No Son means no life¶

    Claim: "Eternal life is in the Son; he that has not the Son has not life" Reference: 1 John 5:11-12 Evidence: John states that God gave eternal life, and this life is in his Son. The person who has the Son has life; the person who does not have the Son does not have life.

    Classification Walkthrough:

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 — οὐκ ἔχει τὴν ζωήν ("has not life") applied to person without the Son. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): ὁ μὴ ἔχων τὸν Υἱόν — generic substantival participle, any literal human without Christ. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): ζωή (G2222, N-ASF) as direct object of ἔχω. ἔδωκεν (δίδωμι G1325, Aor Act Ind 3S) — life is a gift. ἔχει with οὐκ (objective negation) — presently does not possess life. Contingent on having the Son. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Apostolic epistle — direct didactic teaching. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E49, E20, E36. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond.

    E70 — Land of forgetfulness¶

    Claim: "God's wonders are not known in the dark or in the land of forgetfulness" Reference: Psalm 88:10-12 Evidence: The psalmist asks a series of rhetorical questions (all expecting "no") about whether the dead can know God's wonders, experience his lovingkindness, or recall his faithfulness. The realm of the dead is called "destruction" (Abaddon), "darkness," and "the land of forgetfulness."

    Classification Walkthrough:

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 — אֲבַדֹּון ("destruction," v.12), מֵּתִים ("the dead"), רְפָאִים ("shades"), קֶבֶר ("grave"), חֹשֶׁךְ ("darkness"), אֶרֶץ נְשִׁיָּה ("land of oblivion") — cessation-environment terms. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): הַמֵּתִים and רְפָאִים — generic dead humans. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): v.11 — תַּעֲשֶׂה פֶּלֶא (Qal Impf 2ms of עשׂה) with הֲ interrogative expecting "no"; יָקוּמוּ (Qal Impf 3mp of קום) and יוֹדוּךָ (Hiphil Impf 3mp of ידה) — rhetorical questions. v.12 — יְסֻפַּר (Pual Impf 3ms of ספר) in the grave/Abaddon. v.13 — יִוָּדַע (Niphal Impf 3ms of ידע) in darkness. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Lament psalm — direct first-person prayer. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E12-E16. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond.

    N-Item Classifications (Tree 4)¶

    N1 — Nephesh chayyah shared with animals¶

    Claim: "Nephesh chayyah is not an exclusively human attribute — it belongs equally to animals" Based on: E4 (animals are nephesh chayyah — Gen 1:20-21, 24-25)

    Gate 0 (Foundation): - N-Test 1 (Universal agreement): Both sides acknowledge nephesh chayyah applies to animals in Gen 1:20, 24. PASS. - N-Test 2 (No interpretation): Parser confirms נֶפֶשׁ חַיָּה in Gen 1:20, 24 with animals as subject. Only possible conclusion. PASS. - N-Test 3 (Zero added concepts): Restates E4's lexical observation. PASS.

    Vocabulary Scan: No V1 or V2 vocabulary. Neutral.

    Result: Gate 0 passes. Classification: Neutral.

    N2 — Man IS the nephesh, did not receive one¶

    Claim: "The man did not receive a soul; the combined entity (dust + breath) IS the nephesh" Based on: E3 (man became a living soul — transformation verb wayehi — Gen 2:7)

    Gate 0 (Foundation): - N-Test 1 (Universal agreement): Both sides acknowledge wayehi + le-construction means "became," not "received." PASS. - N-Test 2 (No interpretation): Parser confirms יְהִי (היה, Qal.Wayq.3ms) + ל (Prep) + נֶפֶשׁ + חַיָּה. Transformation verb, one grammatical reading. PASS. - N-Test 3 (Zero added concepts): Restates E3 in negative-positive form. PASS.

    Vocabulary Scan: No V1 or V2 vocabulary. Neutral.

    Result: Gate 0 passes. Classification: Neutral.

    N3 — Image of God distinguishes humans from animals¶

    Claim: "What distinguishes humans from animals is not the possession of nephesh or ruach but the image of God" Based on: E4, E5, E23, E10

    Gate 0 (Foundation): - N-Test 1 (Universal agreement): Both sides agree animals share nephesh/ruach; image of God is uniquely human. PASS. - N-Test 2 (No interpretation): Only conclusion from "shared nephesh + shared ruach + unique image." PASS. - N-Test 3 (Zero added concepts): Combines four E-items with no external framework. PASS.

    Vocabulary Scan: No V1 or V2 vocabulary. Neutral.

    Result: Gate 0 passes. Classification: Neutral.

    N4 — God alone has immortality; humans do not¶

    Claim: "If God alone has immortality (E29), no human soul possesses immortality inherently" Based on: E29 (God alone has athanasia — 1 Tim 6:16)

    Gate 0 (Foundation): - N-Test 1 (Universal agreement): "Only X has P" → "non-X lacks P" is tautological. PASS. - N-Test 2 (No interpretation): No alternative conclusion without adding qualifiers. PASS. - N-Test 3 (Zero added concepts): Restates the exclusivity of monos. PASS.

    Vocabulary Scan: V1 — denial of inherent immortality implies mortality. Candidate: COND.

    Validation Gates: - Gate 1 (Subject): God (ho monos echon) with implication for human souls. PASS. - Gate 2 (Grammar): monos (G3441, A-NSM) + echon (G2192, V-PAP-NSM) + athanasian (G110, N-ASF). Exclusive predication, grammatically unqualified. PASS. - Gate 3 (Genre): Pastoral epistle, doxological declaration within didactic instruction. PASS. - Gate 4 (Harmony): No conflict with other E-items. PASS.

    Result: Gate 0 passes. All Tree 3 gates pass. Classification: Cond.

    N5 — Immortality must be acquired at the resurrection¶

    Claim: "Immortality is not presently possessed by humans but must be acquired at the resurrection" Based on: E30 (mortal must put on immortality — 1 Cor 15:53-54), E31 (seek for immortality — Rom 2:7)

    Gate 0 (Foundation): - N-Test 1 (Universal agreement): "Must put on" = "does not currently have." PASS. - N-Test 2 (No interpretation): Only possible conclusion from "seek for" and "must put on." PASS. - N-Test 3 (Zero added concepts): "Acquired at the resurrection" restates 1 Cor 15:53 context. PASS.

    Vocabulary Scan: V1 — θνητός ("mortal") requiring acquisition of ἀθανασία. Candidate: COND.

    Validation Gates: - Gate 1 (Subject): τὸ θνητὸν τοῦτο — literal human bodies. PASS. - Gate 2 (Grammar): δεῖ (V-PAI-3S, necessity) + ἐνδύσασθαι (V-AMN, Aor Mid Inf of ἐνδύω, "to put on"). θνητός (G2349). ζητέω (G2212, V-PAP-DPM, "seeking") + ἀφθαρσίαν (G861). PASS. - Gate 3 (Genre): Didactic epistle (1 Cor 15, Rom 2). PASS. - Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E30, E31. PASS.
