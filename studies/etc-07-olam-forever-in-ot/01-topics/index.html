    Topics Research¶

    Question¶

    Does Hebrew olam (H5769) always mean eternal/endless? All major usage categories including clearly limited uses. Duration determined by nature of subject?

    ETERNITY — GENERAL¶

    • Isa 57:15
    • Mic 5:2
    • Jer 10:10
    • Psa 30:12
    • Psa 41:13
    • Psa 72:17
    • Psa 90:2
    • Psa 110:4
    • Psa 119:142
    • Mat 6:13
    • Mat 18:8
    • 2Co 9:9
    • Jude 1:6

    God inhabits ISA 57:15; MIC 5:2. God rules JER 10:10. See GOD, ETERNITY OF. UNCLASSIFIED SCRIPTURES RELATING TO PSA 30:12; 41:13; 72:17; 90:2; 110:4; 119:142; MAT 6:13; 18:8; 2CO 9:9; Jude 1:6. See LIFE, EVERLASTING. See PUNISHMENT, ETERNAL.

    COVENANT — OF GOD WITH MEN¶

    • Gen 22:16
    • Gen 26:3
    • Gen 8:20-22
    • Gen 9:1-17
    • Psa 105:8
    • Isa 54:10
    • Isa 61:8
    • Lev 26:44-45
    • Deu 4:31
    • Deu 7:8-9
    • Jer 44:26-27
    • Heb 8:9
    • Exo 31:16
    • Exo 34:28
    • Gen 17:1-22
    • Num 25:12-13
    • Jer 31:31-34
    • Heb 8:4-13

    Confirmed with an oath GEN 22:16; 26:3; HEB 6:13,17,18. Binding LEV 26; JER 11:2,3; GAL 3:15. Everlasting GEN 8:20-22; 9:1-17; PSA 105:8,10; ISA 54:10; 61:8. God faithful to LEV 26:44,45; DEU 4:31; 7:8,9. Repudiated by God on account of Jews' idolatry JER 44:26,27; HEB 8:9. Broken by the Jews JER 22:9; EZK 16:59; HEB 8:9. Of the sabbath EXO 31:16. With Phinehas NUM 25:12,13. THE SECOND COVENANT JER 31:31-34; HEB 8:4-13.

    SERVANT — BOND - LAWS¶

    • Exo 21:1-11
    • Lev 25:6
    • Lev 25:10
    • Lev 25:35-55
    • Deu 15:12-18
    • Deu 23:15-16

    Laws of Moses concerning EXO 21:1-11,20,21,26,27,32; LEV 19:20-22; 25:6,10,35-55; DEU 15:12,14,18; 24:7. Voluntary servitude of LEV 25:47; DEU 15:16,17; JOS 9:11-21. Emancipation of EZR 1:1-4; JER 34:8-22; 1CO 7:21.

    PRIEST — HEREDITARY OFFICE¶

    • Exo 28:1-4
    • Exo 29:9
    • Exo 40:15
    • Num 25:13
    • Heb 7:12
    • Heb 7:24

    Hereditary, descent of office EXO 27:21; 28:43; 29:9. Consecration of EXO 29:1-9,19-35; 40:12-16. The Aaronic priesthood called 'everlasting priesthood' (Exo 40:15; Num 25:13) but changed per Heb 7:12.

    FIRE — EVERLASTING FIRE¶

    • Isa 33:14
    • Mat 18:8
    • Mat 25:41
    • Mrk 9:44
    • Rev 20:9
    • Deu 4:24
    • Deu 32:22
    • Mal 3:2

    EVERLASTING FIRE ISA 33:14; MAT 18:8; 25:41; MRK 9:44. Of the destruction of the wicked MAT 13:42,50; 25:41; MRK 9:44; REV 9:2; 21:8. Judgments DEU 4:24; 32:22; ISA 33:14; JER 23:29; AMO 1:4,7,10,12,14; 2:2; MAL 3:2; LUK 12:49; REV 20:9.

    SODOM — DESTRUCTION¶

    • Gen 19:1-29
    • Deu 29:23
    • Isa 13:19
    • Jer 49:18
    • Lam 4:6
    • Amo 4:11
    • 2Pe 2:6
    • Jude 1:7
    • Mat 10:15
    • Luk 17:29

    Destroyed on account of wickedness GEN 19:1-29; DEU 29:23; ISA 13:19; JER 49:18; 50:40; LAM 4:6; AMO 4:11; ZEP 2:9; MAT 10:15; LUK 17:29; ROM 9:29; 2PE 2:6. Wickedness of GEN 13:13; 19:4-13; DEU 32:32; ISA 3:9; JER 23:14; EZK 16:46,48,49; Jude 1:7.

    • Exo 12:24
    • Exo 12:43
    • Exo 13:10
    • Num 9:14
    • Isa 24:5
    • Gal 5:6
    • Gal 6:15
    • Eph 2:15
    • Col 2:14
    • Heb 9:1

    A decree EXO 12:14,24,43; 13:10; 15:25; NUM 9:14; 10:8; 15:15; 18:8; ISA 24:5; MAL 4:4. INSUFFICIENCY OF, FOR SALVATION ISA 1:10-17; GAL 5:6; 6:15; EPH 2:15; COL 2:14,20-23; HEB 9:1,8-10.

    EMANCIPATION — GENERAL¶

    • Lev 25:8-17
    • Jer 34:8-11
    • 2Ch 36:23
    • Ezr 1:1-4

    Of all Jewish servants at the time of jubilee LEV 25:8-17. Proclamation of by Zedekiah JER 34:8-11; by Cyrus 2CH 36:23; EZR 1:1-4.

    JUBILEE — GENERAL¶

    • Lev 25:8-55
    • Lev 27:17-24
    • Num 36:4
    • Isa 61:2
    • Eze 46:17

    Called THE ACCEPTABLE YEAR OF THE LORD ISA 61:2. Called THE YEAR OF LIBERTY EZK 46:17. LAWS CONCERNING LEV 25:8-55; 27:17-24; NUM 36:4.

    JUDGMENT — THE GENERAL¶

    • Ecc 12:14
    • Dan 7:9-10
    • Mat 25:1-46
    • 2Co 5:10
    • Rev 20:11-15

    THE GENERAL 1CH 16:33; JOB 14:17; PSA 9:7; 50:3-6; 96:13; ECC 3:17; 11:9; 12:14; DAN 7:9,10; MAT 25:1-46; ROM 2:5-10; 2CO 5:10; 2TH 1:7,8; HEB 6:2; 9:27; 2PE 3:7,10-12; REV 20:11-15.

    LAND — SALE AND REDEMPTION¶

    • Lev 25:15-16
    • Lev 25:23-33
    • Gen 13:14-17
    • Jer 32:7-16

    Original title to, from God GEN 13:14-17; 15:7; EXO 23:31; LEV 25:23. Sale and redemption of, laws concerning LEV 25:15,16,23-33; 27:17-24; NUM 36:4; JER 32:7-16,25,44; EZK 46:18.
