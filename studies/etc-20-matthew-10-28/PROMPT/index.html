    Bible Study: Matthew 10:28 -- "And fear not them which kill the body, but are not able to kill the soul: but rather fear him which is able to destroy both soul and body in hell."     Question¶

    Matthew 10:28 -- "And fear not them which kill the body, but are not able to kill the soul: but rather fear him which is able to destroy both soul and body in hell." What does this verse teach about the fate of the wicked? Same-author usage analysis of psyche (G5590), apollymi (G622), gehenna (G1067), and phobeo (G5399) in Matthew's Gospel.

    Prior Research Summary¶

    Nine prior studies directly address Matt 10:28 or its key terms. etc6-19-matthew-10-28 (exact predecessor) found: psyche 16x in Matthew (8 'life', 8 'soul'), apollymi ~18x (judgment uses = actual destruction), gehenna 7/12 NT occurrences in Matthew (never paired with basanizo), and the same word-pair psyche+apollymi in 10:39 = 'lose one's life'. etc7-01-what-is-man established nephesh/psyche as destructible composite-entity terms. etc7-05-four-hell-words confirmed gehenna collocates with apollymi 3x and fire 6x but never basanizo. etc7-06-destruction-vocabulary confirmed apollymi (102 NT occ.) as canonical heir to abad via LXX (141 mappings, PMI 6.14). etc7-14-judgment-passages found 0/8 major judgment passages use basanizo for humans. 3am-05-fear-god analyzed phobeo broadly but did not map all Matthean occurrences. Key gaps: (1) complete parser-verified Matthean distribution of all four words, (2) phobeo distribution in Matthew specifically, (3) etc7 methodology with per-item classifier agents.
