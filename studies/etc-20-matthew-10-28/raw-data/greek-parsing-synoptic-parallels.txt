KEY SYNOPTIC COMPARISON:
Matthew 10:28: phobeisthe (V-PNM-2P, Present M/P Imperative) + apollymi (V-AAN, Aor Act Inf)
Luke 12:4-5: phobethete (V-AOM-2P, Aorist Pass Imperative) + apokteino (V-AAN, Aor Act Inf)

Matthew uses PRESENT imperative (ongoing posture of fear); Luke uses AORIST imperative (decisive act)
Matthew escalates from apokteino to apollymi; Luke uses apokteino for both human and divine action
Matthew mentions psyche explicitly; Luke omits psyche entirely
