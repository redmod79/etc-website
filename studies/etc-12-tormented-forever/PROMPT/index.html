    Bible Study: Rev 20:10 -- named subjects (devil, beast, false prophet). Applied to humans or supernatural/symbolic entities? Basanizo (G928) usage. Apply Tree 3 subject gate.¶

    Question¶

    Rev 20:10 -- named subjects (devil, beast, false prophet). Applied to humans or supernatural/symbolic entities? Basanizo (G928) usage. Apply Tree 3 subject gate.

    Prior Research Summary¶

    etc6-12 established that Rev 20:10 names three non-human/symbolic subjects (devil, beast, false prophet), all of which FAIL Tree 3 Gate 1 (Subject Gate). The 'tormented for ever and ever' formula (basanisthesontai eis tous aionas ton aionon) appears once in Scripture, applied only to these three entities, never to humans. Basanizo (G928) has 12 NT occurrences: 7 non-judgment, 5 judgment (all in Revelation's apocalyptic framework or demonic speech). Vocabulary differentiation between subjects is documented: humans = devoured/second death; non-human/symbolic = tormented. The etc6-15 strongest-case study confirmed this as I-B resolved Strong toward conditionalist reading. Canonical word studies exist for G928, G929, G931, G2342, G5578, G1228, G3041, G1404, G4567, and many related terms.

    Focus Areas¶

    1. The Three Named Subjects of Rev 20:10 — Identification: This is the foundational question of the entire study. READ the word studies for G1228-diabolos, G2342-therion, and G5578-pseudoprophetes from the library. For the devil: Rev 12:9 and 20:2 explicitly identify the dragon as 'that old serpent, called the Devil, and Satan' — a supernatural spirit being, not a human. For the beast: Rev 13:1-2 describes a composite creature with seven heads, ten horns, body parts from four Daniel 7 beasts — a symbolic political-religious system, not a literal individual. READ the G2342-therion word study, noting that therion in Revelation is ALWAYS the apocalyptic beast-entity (38/46 occurrences). For the false prophet: Rev 19:20 identifies the false prophet as the one 'that wrought miracles before him' — matching the earth beast of Rev 13:11-17 exactly. READ G5578-pseudoprophetes and note the singular/plural distinction (3x singular in Rev = specific apocalyptic entity; 8x plural elsewhere = generic class of humans). Run greek_parser.py on Rev 20:10, 12:9, 13:1, 13:11, 19:20 to verify morphological forms. The verb basanisthesontai is 3rd person plural future passive, matching all three as compound subject. Verses: Rev 20:10, Rev 12:9, Rev 20:2, Rev 13:1-2, Rev 13:11-17, Rev 19:20, Rev 17:8, Rev 16:13 Strong's: G1228, G2342, G5578, G1404, G4567

    2. Basanizo (G928) Complete NT Usage Analysis — All 12 Occurrences: READ D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G928-basanizo.md — the canonical entry documents all 12 tokens across 6 books. Run greek_parser.py on each occurrence: Matt 4:24 (illness), 8:6 (illness), 8:29 (demons), 14:24 (waves tossing ship), Mark 5:7 (demon), 6:48 (rowing), Luke 8:28 (demon), 2 Pet 2:8 (moral distress), Rev 9:5 (time-limited torment), 11:10 (prophetic witness), 12:2 (childbirth), 14:10 (judgment in apocalyptic), 20:10 (judgment in apocalyptic). The distribution is critical: 7 of 12 are NON-judgment (illness, waves, rowing, distress, childbirth), and the 5 judgment uses are ALL either in Revelation's apocalyptic framework or involve demons speaking of their own future. No epistle, no Gospel didactic section, and no OT passage uses basanizo for the final fate of generic human wicked. Also READ the word studies for G929-basanismos (6 occurrences, ALL in Revelation, 3 describing Babylon's completed destruction) and G931-basanos (3 occurrences — physical diseases and parabolic context). Check G930-basanistes (1 occurrence, Matt 18:34, parabolic). The word family root meaning is 'touchstone for testing metals' — the metaphorical extension to 'torment' is secondary. Verses: Matt 4:24, Matt 8:6, Matt 8:29, Matt 14:24, Mark 5:7, Mark 6:48, Luke 8:28, 2 Pet 2:8, Rev 9:5, Rev 11:10, Rev 12:2, Rev 14:10, Rev 20:10 Strong's: G928, G929, G931, G930

    3. Tree 3 Subject Gate Application to Rev 20:10: The research agent MUST read D:/bible/bible-studies/etc6-series-methodology.md to understand the Tree 3 decision tree. For each of the three subjects: (1) THE DEVIL — Is the devil a literal human being? Rev 12:9 identifies the devil as the dragon, that old serpent — a supernatural spirit being who predates humanity (Gen 3:1-5). Gate 1 automatic FAIL: 'a non-human spirit being.' (2) THE BEAST — Is the beast a literal human being? Rev 13:1 describes a creature with seven heads and ten horns rising from the sea, a composite of Daniel 7's four beasts. Rev 17:8 says the beast 'was, and is not, and shall ascend' — a temporal description impossible for a single human. Gate 1 automatic FAIL: 'a symbolic entity (a figure in an apocalyptic vision).' (3) THE FALSE PROPHET — Is the false prophet a literal human being? READ D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G931-basanos.md — 3 NT occurrences: Matt 4:24 (physical diseases), Luke 16:23 (parabolic torment), Luke 16:28 (parabolic torment).     11. The 'Beast-as-Representative' Counter-Argument: etc6-12 addressed this in its 'What CANNOT Be Said' section. The key question: even if the beast represents a system composed of human adherents, does Rev 20:10's torment formula transfer to those humans? The text's own vocabulary argues NO. Rev 19:20-21 provides the clearest evidence: the beast and false prophet are cast 'alive' (zontes) into the lake, while the human remnant is 'slain' (apektanthesan) by the sword. The text deliberately uses DIFFERENT verbs for the symbolic entities and for their human followers IN THE SAME NARRATIVE. Rev 20:9 likewise describes the human followers of the post-millennium Satan as 'devoured' (katephagen) by fire — destruction vocabulary, not torment vocabulary. Rev 21:8 lists eight categories of human sinners and assigns them 'the second death' — not the torment formula. The text itself maintains the vocabulary distinction even when describing the same event. What CAN be said: the beast represents a system. What CANNOT be said from the text: that the torment formula applied to the symbolic entity automatically transfers to the humans within that system, because the text explicitly applies different vocabulary to them.     12. The 'For Ever and Ever' Formula — Subject-Determined Meaning: READ D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G165-aion.md and G166-aionios.md for the semantic range of the aion word family. The formula 'eis tous aionas ton aionon' (unto the ages of the ages) occurs approximately 13 times in Revelation: 9x for God's existence and reign (genuinely endless: Rev 1:6, 4:9-10, 5:13, 7:12, 10:6, 11:15, 15:7, 22:5), 1x for saints' reign (genuinely endless: Rev 22:5), and 3x for judgment contexts (Rev 14:11, 19:3, 20:10). The crucial observation: the formula's meaning appears SUBJECT-DETERMINED. Applied to the eternal God, it is genuinely eternal. Applied to judgment, the OT parallel in Isa 34:10 (smoke of Edom ascending 'for ever' — yet Edom is not still burning and animals inhabit the ruins, Isa 34:11-17) shows the formula can describe a permanent, irreversible result rather than an ongoing process. Rev 19:3 applies the identical formula to Babylon's smoke, and Rev 18:21-23 describes Babylon as 'found no more at all' with fivefold cessation — the smoke formula describes a completed, permanent destruction. Cross-reference the prior study for-ever-and-ever-revelation which extensively documents this pattern. Verses: Rev 20:10, Rev 14:11, Rev 19:3, Rev 1:6, Rev 4:9-10, Isa 34:9-10, Isa 34:11-17, Rev 18:21-23 Strong's: G165, G166
