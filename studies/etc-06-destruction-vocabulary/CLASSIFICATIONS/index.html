    CLASSIFICATIONS.md — Destruction Vocabulary¶

    Classification Audit Trail¶

    This file contains the full Tree 3/4 walkthroughs for every E-item classified as Cond. or ECT and every N-item in this study.

    E-Item Walkthroughs (Tree 3)¶

    E1 — "The way of the ungodly shall perish (abad)" (Psa 1:6)¶

    Candidate: Cond.

    Step 1 — Vocabulary Scan: V1: "perish" (abad) = destruction/cessation vocabulary applied to the wicked. YES → Candidate: Cond.

    Step 2 — Four Validation Gates:

    Gate 1 (Subject): The grammatical subject is "the way of the ungodly" — this is an abstract concept (a way/path), not a literal human. However, the "way" metonymically represents the people who walk it. The verse contrasts "the righteous" (literal humans, v.6a) with "the ungodly" (literal humans, v.6b). PASS — the subject through metonymy is the ungodly (literal humans).

    Gate 2 (Grammar): abad Qal impf 3fs — intransitive "shall perish." No alternative parsing. The Qal of abad means "to perish" without ambiguity. PASS.

    Gate 3 (Genre): Wisdom poetry (Psalm 1). Didactic in function — direct teaching about the two paths. PASS.

    Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E2 (Psa 37:20), E3 (Psa 37:10), E7 (Psa 92:9), E9 (Psa 145:20), and all other destruction vocabulary E-items. No conflict. PASS.

    Classification: Cond. — CONFIRMED.

    E2 — "The wicked shall perish (abad)... consume (kalah); into smoke shall they consume away" (Psa 37:20)¶

    Candidate: Cond.

    Step 1 — Vocabulary Scan: V1: "perish" (abad) + "consume" (kalah) + "into smoke" = destruction/cessation vocabulary. YES → Candidate: Cond.

    Step 2 — Four Validation Gates:

    Gate 1 (Subject): "The wicked" and "the enemies of the LORD" — literal humans. PASS.

    Gate 2 (Grammar): abad Qal impf 3mp ("shall perish") + kalah Qal perf 3p ("consumed") x2. Standard forms, no alternative parsing. PASS.

    Gate 3 (Genre): Wisdom psalm. Didactic function — direct teaching on the fate of the wicked. PASS.

    Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with all other destruction vocabulary E-items. The smoke simile reinforces cessation. No conflict. PASS.

    Classification: Cond. — CONFIRMED.

    E3 — "Yet a little while, and the wicked shall not be (ayin)" (Psa 37:10)¶

    Candidate: Cond.

    Step 1 — Vocabulary Scan: V1: "shall not be" (ayin) = non-existence/cessation language. YES → Candidate: Cond.

    Step 2 — Four Validation Gates:

    Gate 1 (Subject): "The wicked" — literal humans. PASS.

    Gate 2 (Grammar): ayin rasha = "the wicked is not." ayin = noun of non-existence (H369). einennu = "he is not" with 3ms suffix. No alternative parsing for ayin. PASS.

    Gate 3 (Genre): Wisdom psalm. Didactic. PASS.

    Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E4 (Psa 59:13), E5 (Psa 104:35), E6 (Oba 1:16), and all non-existence statements. No conflict. PASS.

    Classification: Cond. — CONFIRMED.

    E4 — "Consume (kalah) them in wrath, that they may not be (ayin)" (Psa 59:13)¶

    Candidate: Cond.

    Step 1 — Vocabulary Scan: V1: "consume" (kalah) + "may not be" (ayin) = destruction + non-existence. YES → Candidate: Cond.

    Step 2 — Four Validation Gates:

    Gate 1 (Subject): "Them" — refers to enemies/wicked in context. Literal humans. PASS.

    Gate 2 (Grammar): kalah Piel imperative + we'einemo ("and they are not"). Standard forms. PASS.

    Gate 3 (Genre): Imprecatory psalm (prayer). Didactic in its theological content — expresses belief about what consumption produces. PASS.

    Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E3 (Psa 37:10), E5 (Psa 104:35). No conflict. PASS.

    Classification: Cond. — CONFIRMED.

    E5 — "Let the sinners be consumed out of the earth, and let the wicked be no more (ayin)" (Psa 104:35)¶

    Candidate: Cond.

    Step 1 — Vocabulary Scan: V1: "consumed" (tamam) + "be no more" (ayin) = destruction + non-existence. YES → Candidate: Cond.

    Step 2 — Four Validation Gates:

    Gate 1 (Subject): "Sinners" and "the wicked" — literal humans. PASS.

    Gate 2 (Grammar): tamam + ayin. Standard forms. PASS.

    Gate 3 (Genre): Praise psalm with wisdom element. Didactic. PASS.

    Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E3, E4. No conflict. PASS.

    Classification: Cond. — CONFIRMED.

    E6 — "They shall be as though they had not been (kelo hayu)" (Oba 1:16)¶

    Candidate: Cond.

    Step 1 — Vocabulary Scan: V1: "as though they had not been" = cessation/non-existence. YES → Candidate: Cond.

    Step 2 — Four Validation Gates:

    Gate 1 (Subject): "All the heathen" — literal humans/nations. PASS.

    Gate 2 (Grammar): kelo hayu = "as if they had not been." Standard construction. PASS.

    Gate 3 (Genre): Prophetic oracle. Direct-speech prophecy ("Thus saith the LORD" context). PASS.

    Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E3, E4, E5. Strongest cessation formula. No conflict. PASS.

    Classification: Cond. — CONFIRMED.

    E7 — "The wicked shall be destroyed (shamad) for ever" (Psa 92:7)¶

    Candidate: Cond.

    Step 1 — Vocabulary Scan: V1: "destroyed" (shamad) = destruction vocabulary. YES → Candidate: Cond.

    Step 2 — Four Validation Gates:

    Gate 1 (Subject): "The wicked" and "workers of iniquity" — literal humans. PASS.

    Gate 2 (Grammar): shamad Niphal inf + adey-ad. "To be destroyed to perpetuity." No alternative parsing. PASS.

    Gate 3 (Genre): Sabbath psalm with wisdom element. Didactic. PASS.

    Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E8 (shamad in Psa 37:38), E9 (shamad in Psa 145:20). No conflict. PASS.

    Classification: Cond. — CONFIRMED.

    E8 — "The transgressors shall be destroyed (shamad) together: the end of the wicked shall be cut off" (Psa 37:38)¶

    Candidate: Cond.

    Step 1 — Vocabulary Scan: V1: "destroyed" (shamad) + "cut off" (karath) = destruction vocabulary. YES → Candidate: Cond.

    Step 2 — Four Validation Gates:

    Gate 1 (Subject): "Transgressors" and "the wicked" — literal humans. PASS.

    Gate 2 (Grammar): shamad Niphal perf 3p + karath Niphal perf 3fs. Prophetic perfects. Standard. PASS.

    Gate 3 (Genre): Wisdom psalm. Didactic. PASS.

    Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E2, E7, E9. No conflict. PASS.

    Classification: Cond. — CONFIRMED.

    E9 — "The LORD preserveth (shamar) all them that love him: but all the wicked will he destroy (shamad)" (Psa 145:20)¶

    Candidate: Cond.

    Step 1 — Vocabulary Scan: V1: "destroy" (shamad) = destruction vocabulary applied to the wicked. YES → Candidate: Cond.

    Step 2 — Four Validation Gates:

    Gate 1 (Subject): "All the wicked" — literal humans. PASS.

    Gate 2 (Grammar): shamad Hiphil impf 3ms. Causative active — God actively destroys. No alternative parsing. PASS.

    Gate 3 (Genre): Acrostic praise psalm with didactic content. PASS.

    Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E7, E8, and all destruction vocabulary E-items. The shamar/shamad contrast reinforces: preservation vs. destruction. No conflict. PASS.

    Classification: Cond. — CONFIRMED.

    E10 — "He shall perish (abad) for ever like his own dung" (Job 20:7)¶

    Candidate: Cond.

    Step 1 — Vocabulary Scan: V1: "perish" (abad) + "for ever" = destruction + permanence. YES → Candidate: Cond.

    Step 2 — Four Validation Gates:

    Gate 1 (Subject): "He" — the wicked man described in Job 20. Literal human. PASS.

    Gate 2 (Grammar): abad Qal impf 3ms + lanetsach. Standard. PASS.

    Gate 3 (Genre): Wisdom discourse (Job's friend Zophar speaking). Didactic. PASS.

    Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E1, E2, E7. No conflict. PASS.

    Classification: Cond. — CONFIRMED.

    E11 — "Broad is the way that leadeth to destruction (apoleia); narrow the way to life" (Matt 7:13-14)¶

    Candidate: Cond.

    Step 1 — Vocabulary Scan: V1: "destruction" (apoleia) = destruction vocabulary. YES → Candidate: Cond.

    Step 2 — Four Validation Gates:

    Gate 1 (Subject): People who enter the gate — literal humans. PASS.

    Gate 2 (Grammar): apoleia as destination noun. Standard. PASS.

    Gate 3 (Genre): Sermon on the Mount — didactic teaching. PASS.

    Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E12 (John 3:16), E14 (Rom 6:23), E15 (Phil 3:19). No conflict. PASS.

    Classification: Cond. — CONFIRMED.

    E12 — "Should not perish (apollymi) but have everlasting life" (John 3:16)¶

    Candidate: Cond.

    Step 1 — Vocabulary Scan: V1: "perish" (apollymi) = destruction/cessation vocabulary. YES → Candidate: Cond.

    Step 2 — Four Validation Gates:

    Gate 1 (Subject): "Whosoever believeth" (and by implication, whosoever does not believe) — literal humans. PASS.

    Gate 2 (Grammar): apollymi Aor Mid Subj 3s in purpose clause (hina me). The antithesis is structural: me apoletai vs. eche zoen aionion. No alternative parsing. PASS.

    Gate 3 (Genre): Gospel narrative — Jesus' direct teaching (didactic). PASS.

    Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E11, E13, E14, and all perish-vs-life passages. No conflict. PASS.

    Classification: Cond. — CONFIRMED.

    E13 — "Except ye repent, ye shall all likewise perish (apollymi)" (Luke 13:3,5)¶

    Candidate: Cond.

    Step 1 — Vocabulary Scan: V1: "perish" (apollymi) = destruction vocabulary. YES → Candidate: Cond.

    Step 2 — Four Validation Gates:

    Gate 1 (Subject): "Ye" — Jesus' audience, literal humans. PASS.

    Gate 2 (Grammar): apollymi Fut Mid Ind 2p. Standard. PASS.

    Gate 3 (Genre): Direct teaching (didactic). PASS.

    Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E12, E11. No conflict. PASS.

    Classification: Cond. — CONFIRMED.

    E14 — "The wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life" (Rom 6:23)¶

    Candidate: Cond.

    Step 1 — Vocabulary Scan: V1: "death" (thanatos) as the penalty for sin = cessation vocabulary. YES → Candidate: Cond.

    Step 2 — Four Validation Gates:

    Gate 1 (Subject): Sinners generally — literal humans. PASS.

    Gate 2 (Grammar): thanatos (death) vs. zoe aionios (eternal life). Standard construction. No alternative parsing. PASS.

    Gate 3 (Genre): Pauline epistle — didactic teaching. PASS.

    Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E12 (perish vs. life), E11 (destruction vs. life). No conflict. PASS.

    Classification: Cond. — CONFIRMED.

    E15 — "Whose end (telos) is destruction (apoleia)" (Phil 3:19)¶

    Candidate: Cond.

    Step 1 — Vocabulary Scan: V1: "destruction" (apoleia) = destruction vocabulary. YES → Candidate: Cond.

    Step 2 — Four Validation Gates:

    Gate 1 (Subject): "Enemies of the cross of Christ" — literal humans. PASS.

    Gate 2 (Grammar): telos + apoleia. Standard. PASS.

    Gate 3 (Genre): Pauline epistle — didactic. PASS.

    Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E11, E14. No conflict. PASS.

    Classification: Cond. — CONFIRMED.

    E16 — "Punished with everlasting destruction (olethros aionios) from the presence of the Lord" (2 Thess 1:9)¶

    Candidate: Cond.

    Step 1 — Vocabulary Scan: V1: "destruction" (olethros) = destruction vocabulary. YES → Candidate: Cond.

    Step 2 — Four Validation Gates:

    Gate 1 (Subject): "Them that know not God, and that obey not the gospel" (v.8) — literal humans. PASS.

    Gate 2 (Grammar): olethron aionion. Both accusative. aionios modifies olethros (destruction noun). apo prosopou = "from the presence" — grammatically can mean "away from" (separation) or "proceeding from" (source).
