    Word Studies¶

    Question¶

    Rev 14:9-11, 19:3 -- OT source imagery (Isa 34:9-10, Gen 19:28, Mal 4:1-3). Ongoing torment vs. permanent destruction? Apply Tree 3 genre gate.

    G929 — basanismos¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G929-basanismos.md

    Occurrences: 6

    Masculine noun meaning torment — from G928. All 6 NT occurrences in Revelation: Rev 9:5 (2x, five-month limit), Rev 14:11, Rev 18:7, Rev 18:10, Rev 18:15. Three of six describe Babylon's completed destruction (18:7,10,15). The word is exclusively apocalyptic in distribution.

    Semantic categories: demonic/supernatural torment, eschatological judgment, commercial/imperial torment

    Key verses: Rev 14:11, Rev 9:5, Rev 18:7, Rev 18:10, Rev 18:15

    Relevance: Core noun in the crux text Rev 14:11. Its distribution is decisive: 6 occurrences, all Revelation, 3/6 = Babylon's completed destruction, 1/6 has five-month time limit. Only the crux text is debated.

    G928 — basanizo¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G928-basanizo.md

    Occurrences: 12

    Verb meaning to torment/test. 12 NT occurrences: 7 non-judgment (illness Matt 8:6, demonic speech Matt 8:29/Mark 5:7/Luke 8:28, waves Matt 14:24, rowing Mark 6:48, Lot vexed 2 Pet 2:8), 5 Revelation apocalyptic (Rev 9:5, 11:10, 12:2 labor pains, 14:10, 20:10). All judgment uses are in apocalyptic framework.

    Semantic categories: physical suffering, demonic encounters, moral distress, eschatological torment, labor pains, natural forces

    Key verses: Rev 14:10, Rev 20:10, Matt 8:29, Matt 14:24, 2 Pet 2:8

    Relevance: Verb form in Rev 14:10 (basanisthesetai, future passive). Its semantic range includes non-torment uses (waves, rowing, labor pains). All 5 judgment uses are in Revelation's apocalyptic genre.

    G2586 — kapnos¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G2586-kapnos.md

    Occurrences: 13

    Masculine noun meaning smoke. 13 tokens in 11 verses — Acts 2:19 (quoting Joel) + 10 Revelation occurrences. Used for incense smoke (8:4), abyss smoke (9:2-3), judgment triad fire+smoke+brimstone (9:17-18), smoke of torment (14:11), temple smoke (15:8), Babylon's burning (18:9,18), Babylon's smoke rising forever (19:3).

    Semantic categories: prophetic sign, incense ascending, abyss smoke, judgment instrument, perpetual rising/evidence, divine glory, Babylon's destruction

    Key verses: Rev 14:11, Rev 19:3, Rev 18:9, Rev 18:18, Rev 9:2

    Relevance: Central imagery word in both crux texts (14:11, 19:3). OT source via ashan (H6227). In OT, smoke consistently represents aftermath/evidence of completed destruction or transience/vanishing.

    G372 — anapausis¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G372-anapausis.md

    Occurrences: 5

    Feminine noun meaning rest/intermission/cessation. 5 NT occurrences: Matt 11:29 (rest for souls), Matt 12:43/Luke 11:24 (unclean spirit seeking rest), Rev 4:8 (living creatures rest not day and night), Rev 14:11 (beast-worshippers have no rest day and night). From anapauo G373 (to cause to rest).

    Semantic categories: spiritual rest offered by Christ, rest sought by unclean spirits, ceaseless activity, absence of rest as punishment

    Key verses: Rev 14:11, Rev 4:8, Matt 11:29

    Relevance: Rev 14:11 and Rev 4:8 use identical construction (ouk echousin anapausin hemeras kai nuktos). In 4:8, it characterizes uninterrupted activity during occurrence, not eternal duration. Same-book same-author parallel is the strongest interpretive evidence.

    G165 — aion¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G165-aion.md

    Occurrences: 120

    Masculine noun meaning age/eon/perpetuity/world. ~120 tokens across 95 verses in 22 NT books. The 'ages of ages' formula (eis tous aionas ton aionon) occurs ~21 times: ~18 doxological (genuinely eternal — God's glory, existence, reign), 3 judgment (Rev 14:11, 19:3, 20:10 — all in Revelation's apocalyptic framework).

    Semantic categories: duration/perpetuity, intensified perpetuity (ages of ages), this present age/world, the age to come, time-span/epochs, created order/universe, end of the age

    Key verses: Rev 14:11, Rev 19:3, Rev 20:10, Rev 4:9-10, Gal 1:5

    Relevance: The 'ages of ages' formula is the crux of the 'forever' claim in Rev 14:11. Its 3 judgment uses are ALL in Revelation's apocalyptic framework, all applied to entities whose destruction is described as complete elsewhere. The 18 doxological uses describe genuinely eternal realities because God is genuinely eternal.

    G166 — aionios¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G166-aionios.md

    Occurrences: 71

    Adjective meaning eternal/age-long/pertaining to the age(s). 71 tokens in 69 verses across 19 books. Dominant collocation: 'eternal life' (~43x). Duration determined by the nature of the subject. Three past-time uses (Rom 16:25; 2 Tim 1:9; Tit 1:2) falsify 'always means endless future.' Aidios (G126) exists as the strict eternality word.

    Semantic categories: eternal life as gift, eternal judgment/punishment, eternal attributes of God, eternal realities, eternal purpose/pre-temporal reference, eternal kingdom

    Key verses: John 3:16, Matt 25:46, 2 Thess 1:9, Jude 1:7

    Relevance: Modified by judgment nouns: kolasin aionion (Matt 25:46), olethron aionion (2 Thess 1:9), puros aioniou (Jude 1:7). The Hebrews pattern (eternal redemption, eternal salvation = permanent results of completed acts) governs interpretation. Aionios never modifies basanismos anywhere in Scripture.

    H5769 — olam¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/H5769-owlam.md

    Occurrences: 432

    Masculine noun meaning concealed time/time out of mind/forever. 432 tokens across 406 verses in 34 books. From root alam (H5956, to conceal). Semantic range: three days (Jonah 2:6) to genuinely eternal (Psa 90:2). Duration determined by subject: God = eternal; mortal subjects = lifetime; fire/smoke = completed judgments. Isa 34:10 uses le-olam for smoke that ended (animals dwell in ruins, vv.11-17).

    Semantic categories: eternal duration of God, everlasting covenant, perpetual cultic ordinances, the immemorial past, eschatological/age to come, permanent judgment/irreversible desolation

    Key verses: Isa 34:10, Psa 90:2, Josh 8:28, Jer 25:9

    Relevance: Governs Isa 34:10 — the primary OT source for Rev 14:11. Le-olam ya'aleh ashanah ('its smoke shall ascend forever') is the exact formula Revelation draws on. Yet the olam fire ended: animals inhabit the territory (Isa 34:11-17). Olam fire = permanent result, not endless process.

    H5331 — netsach¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/H5331-netsach.md

    Occurrences: 44

    Masculine noun meaning perpetuity/victory/strength. 44 occurrences. The le-netsach netsachim form in Isa 34:10 is the STRONGEST Hebrew 'forever' form (construct + plural, intensifying). Yet even this strongest form describes an ended fire. Four passages (Psa 103:9; Isa 57:16; Jer 3:5; Psa 77:7-8) explicitly DENY perpetuity of divine wrath using netsach in parallelism with olam.

    Semantic categories: perpetual duration, denial of perpetual divine wrath, victory/strength/eminence, finality/completeness

    Key verses: Isa 34:10, Psa 103:9, Isa 57:16

    Relevance: The le-netsach netsachim in Isa 34:10 is the strongest Hebrew perpetuity expression — yet the fire ended. Four netsach-olam parallelism passages explicitly deny perpetual divine wrath.

    H6227 — ashan¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/H6227-ashan.md

    Occurrences: 25

    Masculine noun meaning smoke. 25 occurrences across 13 OT books. Semantic categories: theophanic smoke (Gen 15:17, Exod 19:18, Isa 6:4), divine wrath/judgment (2 Sam 22:9, Isa 34:10), transience/vanishing (Psa 37:20, Psa 102:3, Hos 13:3), military signal/destruction evidence (Josh 8:20-21, Judg 20:38,40), eschatological sign (Joel 2:30). LXX maps to kapnos (G2586) with PMI 9.95

    Semantic categories: theophanic smoke, divine wrath and judgment, transience and vanishing, military signal and destruction, eschatological sign, irritant/nuisance

    Key verses: Gen 19:28, Isa 34:10, Psa 37:20, Josh 8:20-21, Hos 13:3

    Relevance: OT source vocabulary for kapnos (G2586). No OT use of ashan describes an ongoing conscious process. Smoke consistently represents aftermath/evidence of completed destruction or transience/vanishing.

    H1614 — gophrith¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/H1614-gophriyth.md

    Occurrences: 7

    Feminine noun meaning brimstone/sulfur. 7 OT occurrences across 6 books. Semantic categories: divine judgment on cities (Gen 19:24, Deut 29:23), eschatological judgment (Ezek 38:22, Isa 34:9), individual retribution (Job 18:15, Psa 11:6), prepared place of burning (Isa 30:33). LXX maps to theion (G2303) with PMI 11.01

    Semantic categories: divine judgment on cities, eschatological judgment imagery, individual retribution, prepared place of burning

    Key verses: Gen 19:24, Deut 29:23, Isa 34:9, Isa 30:33, Ezek 38:22

    Relevance: Hebrew source for theion (G2303). Every OT brimstone occurrence describes destruction, not ongoing torment. The fire-and-brimstone paradigm consistently produces permanent destruction.

    G2303 — theion¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G2303-theion.md

    Occurrences: 7

    Neuter noun meaning brimstone/sulfur. 7 NT occurrences: Luke 17:29 (Sodom — 'destroyed them all') + 6 in Revelation (9:17, 9:18, 14:10, 19:20, 20:10, 21:8). Etymologically connected to theios (divine) — 'divine substance.' Co-occurs with pyr (fire) 6 times. Every usage is judgment-related.

    Semantic categories: Sodom judgment archetype, eschatological torment, lake of fire imagery, demonic warfare imagery

    Key verses: Rev 14:10, Rev 21:8, Rev 19:20, Luke 17:29

    Relevance: The Greek equivalent of gophrith (H1614). In Rev 14:10, fire and brimstone = the Sodom paradigm applied eschatologically. Luke 17:29 interprets the paradigm with apollymi (destroyed), not basanizo.

    G4591 — semaino¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G4591-semaino.md

    Occurrences: 6

    Verb meaning to signify/indicate through signs. 6 NT occurrences: John 12:33, 18:32, 21:19 (signifying manner of death), Acts 11:28 (prophetic revelation), Acts 25:27 (formal communication), Rev 1:1 (divine apocalyptic communication). All Johannine uses involve indirect, symbolic communication.

    Semantic categories: indicating manner of death, prophetic revelation, formal communication, divine apocalyptic communication

    Key verses: Rev 1:1

    Relevance: Rev 1:1's esemainen establishes Revelation as sign-communicated — symbolic imagery, not literal plain-language description. Foundation for Tree 3 genre gate.

    G622 — apollymi¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G622-apollymi.md

    Occurrences: 102

    Verb meaning to destroy/perish/lose. ~102 tokens across 86 verses in 15 NT books. Primary destruction verb. Active: destroy (Matt 10:28 — destroy soul and body in Gehenna). Middle/passive: perish (John 3:16 — not perish but have eternal life). LXX primary equivalent of abad (H6) with 141 co-occurrences (PMI 6.14). Used across ALL NT genres by ALL authors. Luke 17:29: Jesus says fire 'destroyed them all' for Sodom.

    Semantic categories: physical destruction/killing, divine judgment/eschatological ruin, perishing in eternal judgment, losing one's life, losing a possession, decay/perishability

    Key verses: Matt 10:28, John 3:16, Luke 17:29, 2 Pet 3:9

    Relevance: The primary NT verb for human fate. Used by Jesus (Matt 10:28 — destroy soul and body), John (3:16 — perish vs. life), Paul (1 Cor 1:18 — them that perish), Peter (2 Pet 3:9 — willing that none perish), Luke (17:29 — destroyed them all). Destruction vocabulary for human fate across all authors and genres.

    G2288 — thanatos¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G2288-thanatos.md

    Occurrences: 119

    Masculine noun meaning death. ~119 tokens across 106 verses in 15 NT books. In Revelation: personified rider (6:8), 'second death' (20:14, 21:8), 'no more death' (21:4). The 'second death' is the lake of fire's designation for human wicked. Death is the antonym of life (zoe G2222).

    Semantic categories: physical death, death as consequence of sin, death as reigning power, Christ's atoning death, spiritual death, death personified/second death, shadow of death

    Key verses: Rom 6:23, Rev 20:14, Rev 21:8, Rev 21:4

    Relevance: The lake of fire = 'second death' (Rev 20:14, 21:8). When humans enter the lake of fire, the term is death, not torment. The wages of sin is death (Rom 6:23), not eternal torment.

    G4442 — pyr¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G4442-pyr.md

    Occurrences: 74

    Neuter noun meaning fire. ~74 tokens across 73 verses in 14 NT books. Revelation dominates (25/73). Semantic range: eschatological punishment (Matt 25:41, Rev 20:14-15), judgment parables (Matt 3:10, 13:40), divine theophany (Heb 12:29, Acts 2:3), cosmic plagues (Rev 8:5,7), testing/refining (1 Pet 1:7), figurative (Jas 3:5-6). LXX primary equivalent of esh (H784) with 316 co-occurrences.

    Semantic categories: eschatological punishment, judgment parables, divine theophany, cosmic plagues, testing/refining, figurative

    Key verses: Rev 14:10, Rev 20:9, Matt 25:41, Heb 12:29

    Relevance: Fire in Rev 14:10 draws on the Sodom fire-and-brimstone paradigm. Fire in Rev 20:9 'devoured' human enemies. 'Our God is a consuming fire' (Heb 12:29) — fire from God's presence consumes.

    H784 — esh¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/H784-esh.md

    Occurrences: 400

    Feminine noun meaning fire. ~400 tokens across 348 verses in 33 OT books. Primary Hebrew fire word. Semantic categories: literal combustion, altar/sacrificial fire, divine theophany (Exod 3:2, Deut 4:12), judgment/devouring/destruction (Gen 19:24, Amos 1:10, Zeph 3:8), metaphor/radiance. Co-occurs with akal (H398, devour) 90 times — fire DEVOURS/CONSUMES. LXX maps to pyr (G4442) with 316 co-occurrences.

    Semantic categories: literal combustion, altar/sacrificial fire, divine theophany, judgment/devouring/destruction, metaphor/radiance

    Key verses: Gen 19:24, Isa 34:9, Mal 4:1

    Relevance: OT source vocabulary for pyr (G4442). The dominant collocation is fire + akal (devour) = fire CONSUMES. Fire in judgment contexts produces destruction, not preservation in torment.

    H665 — epher¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/H665-epher.md

    Occurrences: 22

    Masculine noun meaning ashes. 22 occurrences across 13 books. Semantic categories: dust-and-ashes humility (Gen 18:27, Job 42:6), mourning/distress (2 Sam 13:19, Est 4:1), ritual ashes (Num 19:9-10), worthlessness (Job 13:12), final reduction/destruction (Ezek 28:18 — brought to ashes, Mal 4:3 — wicked become ashes under feet).

    Semantic categories: dust-and-ashes humility, mourning/distress, ritual ashes, worthlessness, final reduction/destruction

    Key verses: Mal 4:3, Ezek 28:18

    Relevance: Mal 4:3: the stated RESULT of eschatological fire is ashes. Ezek 28:18: the being also described in Rev 20:10 is 'brought to ashes.' Ashes = the residue of complete consumption.

    G1799 — enopion¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G1799-enopion.md

    Occurrences: 97

    Preposition meaning in the face of/before/in the presence of. 97 NT occurrences — Revelation dominates with 36 (37%). Semantic categories: divine presence/worship/throne (Rev 4:5, 4:10, 7:9, 7:15, 8:2, 11:16, 14:3, 20:12), divine evaluation/moral standing (Luke 1:6, Rom 3:20, Heb 4:13), witness/testimony (Acts 6:6, 1 Tim 6:12), divine judgment/accountability (Rev 14:10, 16:19, 20:12), angelic intermediaries (Luke 1:19, Rev 8:2).

    Semantic categories: divine presence — worship and throne, divine evaluation — moral/spiritual standing, witness and testimony, divine judgment — accountability, angelic and heavenly intermediaries, interpersonal — before human authorities

    Key verses: Rev 14:10, Rev 4:5, Rev 7:9, Rev 20:12

    Relevance: In Rev 14:10, the torment occurs enopion the holy angels and the Lamb — throne-room/judgment language. Enopion consistently marks public events before a throne/authority, not ongoing states. The implication: the torment is a public judgment execution event, not an eternal condition.

    G2719 — katesthio¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G2719-katesthio.md

    Occurrences: 20

    Verb meaning to eat down/devour. 20 occurrences across 7 NT books. Semantic categories: literal eating (Matt 13:4, Rev 10:9-10), fire consuming/destruction (Rev 11:5, 20:9), figurative exploitation (Mark 12:40, 2 Cor 11:20), consuming zeal (John 2:17), hostile intent (Rev 12:4). LXX maps from H398 akal (eat/devour) paired with H784 esh (fire) — fire devours.

    Semantic categories: literal consumption, fire consuming/destruction, figurative exploitation, consuming zeal, hostile intent

    Key verses: Rev 20:9, Rev 11:5

    Relevance: Rev 20:9: fire katephagen (devoured) the human enemies — destruction vocabulary. The NEXT verse (20:10) uses basanizo (torment) for non-human entities. Different vocabulary for different subjects.

    G305 — anabaino¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G305-anabaino.md

    Occurrences: 86

    Verb meaning to ascend/go up. Used in Rev 14:11 (smoke ascends) and Rev 19:3 (her smoke rose up). Present active indicative in both verses — describes the ascending action as characteristic/ongoing during the vision.

    Semantic categories: ascending motion

    Key verses: Rev 14:11, Rev 19:3

    Relevance: The verb for smoke 'ascending' in both crux texts. Present tense characterizes the vision, not necessarily eternal duration.
