    Prior Research Summary¶

    This is a re-run of etc6-14-judgment-passages under etc7 with stricter classifier methodology. The etc6-14 study produced 31 E-items (7 Cond., 0 ECT, 24 Neutral), 3 N-items (all Neutral), 8 I-items (3 I-A Cond., 2 I-B ECT-direction both resolved Strong toward Cond., 2 I-C ECT-direction, 1 I-D ECT-direction). Key vocabulary patterns established: 4 of 8 passages use explicit destruction/death vocabulary, 2 use penalty/judgment vocabulary without specifying nature, 2 describe the dead viewed with abhorrence, 0 of 8 use basanizo for human wicked. Prior studies etc7-01 through etc7-13 have established extensive vocabulary data, anthropological premises, and classification patterns. The aionios study (etc7-08), olam study (etc7-07), destruction vocabulary study (etc7-06), four hell words (etc7-05), biblical death (etc7-03), immortality (etc7-02), anthropology (etc7-01), smoke ascending (etc7-11), tormented forever (etc7-12), and lake of fire (etc7-13) all provide directly relevant established E-tier and N-tier findings.

    Primary Verses (35 — full analysis required)¶

    Eight Major Judgment Passages: The 8 primary passages to be analyzed individually for E/N/I classification - Rev 20:11-15 — Primary passage — great white throne judgment: books opened, book of life, lake of fire = second death - Matt 25:31-46 — Primary passage — sheep and goats judgment; kolasin aionion vs zoen aionion in v.46 - Dan 12:2 — Primary passage — dual-outcome resurrection: chayyei olam vs dera'on olam; construct chain parallelism - Isa 66:22-24 — Primary passage — new heavens/earth juxtaposed with corpses (peger) viewed with abhorrence (dera'on); worm and fire on dead bodies - John 5:28-29 — Primary passage — resurrection of life vs resurrection of krisis (judgment/damnation) - 2 Thess 1:7-10 — Primary passage — Lord revealed in flaming fire, olethron aionion, apo prosopou debate - Heb 10:26-31 — Primary passage — willful sin, no more sacrifice, fiery indignation, sorer punishment (timoria) - Rom 2:5-11 — Primary passage — day of wrath, righteous judgment, eternal life for patience vs wrath for disobedient

    Immediate Context Passages: Verses immediately surrounding or critical to interpreting the 8 major passages - Rev 20:9-10 — Immediate context — fire devours armies (humans) vs. devil tormented (non-human); vocabulary differentiation - Rev 20:14 — Predicate nominative: lake of fire IS the second death — identification word is thanatos, not basanismos - Rev 21:8 — Second death reiterated — parallel identification of lake of fire; list of sinners who receive it - Matt 25:41 — Fire prepared for the devil and his angels — designation of fire's intended subjects - Dan 12:1 — Context — Michael stands, time of trouble, book of life; deliverance for those written in the book - Isa 66:15-17 — Context — LORD comes with fire and sword to judge; slain and consumed (kalah); precedes v.22-24 - John 5:22 — Context — all judgment committed to the Son - 2 Thess 1:9 — Key verse — everlasting destruction (olethron aionion) from the presence (apo prosopou) of the Lord - Heb 10:27 — Fiery indignation (pyros zelos) that shall devour (esthio) adversaries — destruction vocabulary - Heb 10:29 — Sorer punishment (timoria) — only NT use; how much sorer than death without mercy - Heb 10:39 — Contrast: apoleia (perdition/destruction) vs. saving of the soul — binary outcome - Rom 2:7 — Seeking (zeteo) immortality (aphtharsia) — present active participle implies not yet possessed - Rom 2:12 — Sinners without law shall perish (apollymi) without law — destruction verb for final outcome

    Fire-and-Brimstone Paradigm: OT fire judgment precedents that establish the interpretive framework - Isa 34:9-10 — Edom fire paradigm — smoke ascending forever (olam) for a historically ended fire - Mal 4:1-3 — Day that burns as an oven — wicked become stubble, burned up, ashes under feet of righteous - 2 Pet 2:6 — Sodom turned to ashes — ensample to those that should live ungodly; fire paradigm precedent

    Perish-vs-Life Binary: Core binary framework passages: destruction/death vs eternal life - John 3:16 — Perish (apollymi) vs everlasting life — the perish-vs-life binary in Gospel's most cited verse - Matt 7:13-14 — Broad way to destruction (apoleia) vs narrow way to life (zoe) — binary outcome framework - Rom 6:23 — Wages of sin is death (thanatos), gift of God is eternal life — death-vs-life binary - Matt 10:28 — Fear Him who can destroy (apollymi) both soul (psyche) and body in gehenna — destructibility of soul - Ezek 18:4 — The soul (nephesh) that sins, it shall die — mortality of the soul; divine death sentence on sinners

    Additional Primary Verses: - Heb 10:31 — Fearful thing to fall into hands of the living God — severity emphasis - Rom 2:6-8 — According to deeds: eternal life vs indignation (thumos) and wrath (orge) - Rom 2:9 — Tribulation (thlipsis) and anguish (stenochoria) upon every soul that does evil - Rev 14:9-11 — Smoke of torment ascending — fire-and-brimstone paradigm; cross-reference with Isa 34:10 - Mark 9:43-48 — Gehenna reference quoting Isa 66:24 — worm not die, fire not quenched; subjects are corpses per source text - Rev 19:20 — Beast and false prophet cast alive into lake of fire — symbolic entities; vocabulary for non-human subjects
