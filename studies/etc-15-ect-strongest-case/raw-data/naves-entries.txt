=== WICKED (PEOPLE) — PUNISHMENT OF ===
Extensive entry with hundreds of verse references from Genesis through Revelation.
Key eschatological punishment refs: Dan 12:2; Mat 25:41,46; Mrk 9:43; 2Th 1:9;
Rev 14:9-11; 20:10,15; 21:8. Cross-refs: JUDGMENTS, HELL, PUNISHMENT.

=== WICKED (PEOPLE) — COMPARED WITH ===
All comparisons describe things that cease to exist:
- Chaff (Job 21:18; Psa 1:4; Mat 3:12) — blown away
- Stubble (Job 21:18; Mal 4:1) — burned up
- Ashes under feet (Mal 4:3) — consumed residue
- Melting wax (Psa 68:2) — dissolved
- Morning clouds / early dew / smoke (Hos 13:3) — vanish
- Fire of thorns (Psa 118:12) — flash and gone
- Grass (Psa 37:2; 92:7) — withers
- Tares (Mat 13:38) — burned

=== IMMORTALITY ===
General scriptures: Gen 5:24 through Rev 22:5.
Key: Rom 2:7 (seeking immortality); 1Co 15:53 (mortal puts on immortality);
1Ti 6:16 (God alone has immortality); 2Ti 1:9,10 (brought to light through gospel).
