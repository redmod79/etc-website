    Bible Study: 8 key ECT arguments evaluated at E/N/I tier: Rev 14:9-11, Rev 20:10, Matt 25:46, Mark 9:43-48, Luke 16:19-31, Rev 6:9-11, 2 Thess 1:9, Dan 12:2.¶

    Question¶

    8 key ECT arguments evaluated at E/N/I tier: Rev 14:9-11, Rev 20:10, Matt 25:46, Mark 9:43-48, Luke 16:19-31, Rev 6:9-11, 2 Thess 1:9, Dan 12:2.

    Prior Research Summary¶

    This is a SYNTHESIS study compiling and cross-referencing findings from 13 prior etc7 studies. The reference agent (00-references.md) documents that across all 6 primary studies covering the 8 passages, there are 62 E-tier Cond. items, 0 E-tier ECT items, 139 E-tier Neutral items, and 31 N-items. All 11 I-B items resolved toward Cond. (10 Strong, 1 Moderate). The ECT case for all 8 passages exists entirely at the inference level. The predecessor study (etc6-15) found the same pattern: 0 ECT E-items, 0 ECT N-items, all ECT items at I-tier. Key vocabulary findings: basanizo/basanismos for human eschatological fate appears in zero epistles, zero Gospel teachings, zero OT passages — only in Revelation's apocalyptic framework. Destruction/death vocabulary spans all genres and all major biblical authors.
