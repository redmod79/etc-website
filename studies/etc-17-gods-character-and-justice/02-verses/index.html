    Verses¶

    Question¶

    God's love, justice, holiness, mercy, wrath. Proportionality of punishment. Consistency of infinite torment vs. destruction with biblical justice.

    EXO 34:6¶

    Notes: God's self-description formula. Six mercy attributes: (1) rachum/compassionate, (2) channun/gracious, (3) erek appayim/slow to anger, (4) rab chesed/abundant in lovingkindness, (5) emeth/truth/faithfulness. Mercy attributes dominate.

    EXO 34:7¶

    Notes: Mercy extends to 'thousands' (la-alaphim); judgment limited to 'third and fourth generation' (shilleshim, ribbeeim). Ratio: thousands vs 3-4. Mercy vastly outweighs judgment in God's self-revelation.

    EXO 34:6-7¶

    Notes: Combined divine name formula. Echoed in Num 14:18, Neh 9:17, Psa 86:15, 103:8, 145:8, Joel 2:13, Jonah 4:2, Nah 1:3. The most frequently echoed self-description in the OT. Mercy attributes outnumber judgment attributes 6:2.

    PSA 103:8¶

    Notes: Direct echo of Exo 34:6. Uses rachum, channun, erek appayim, rab chesed.

    PSA 103:9¶

    Notes: Explicit statement: God will NOT keep anger le-olam (forever). Part of the 'not keep anger forever' constellation with Isa 57:16, Jer 3:5, Mic 7:18.

    PSA 103:10¶

    Notes: God does NOT give proportional punishment for every sin. Mercy restrains strict justice.

    PSA 103:11¶

    Notes: Spatial metaphor for the magnitude of chesed — heaven-to-earth distance.

    PSA 103:12¶

    Notes: East-to-west — an immeasurable distance. Complete removal of transgression.

    PSA 103:13¶

    Notes: Parental compassion metaphor. racham = womb-love compassion.

    PSA 103:14¶

    Notes: Anthropological awareness: God remembers human frailty (dust = aphar, Gen 2:7, 3:19). Connected to Gen 3:19.

    PSA 103:8-14¶

    Notes: Extended echo of Exo 34:6-7. Adds 'not always chide', 'not keep anger forever', 'not dealt with us after our sins', 'remembers we are dust'. God's mercy explicitly dominates His dealings.

    MIC 7:18¶

    Notes: Retains not anger le-olam. Delights in chesed. God's pleasure is in mercy, not in judgment.

    LAM 3:33¶

    Notes: Hebrew milibo = 'from his heart'. God does NOT afflict from His heart. Judgment is not God's natural inclination. Piel of anah (afflict) + negation + milibo.

    LAM 3:31-33¶

    Notes: Triple statement: (1) not cast off forever, (2) will have compassion, (3) does not afflict from his heart.

    ISA 57:16¶

    Notes: God gives an ANTHROPOLOGICAL REASON for limiting wrath: human spirits would fail (yaatoph = faint/be overwhelmed). He limits His own wrath because of what humans ARE (mortal beings He made). lo le-olam ariv = not contend forever. lo la-netsach eqtsoph = not be angry perpetually.

    JER 3:5¶

    Notes: Rhetorical question expecting answer 'no'. Will God keep anger le-olam? The expected answer based on Exo 34:6-7 is no.

    PSA 30:5¶

    Notes: Temporal asymmetry: anger = rega (moment); favor = chayyim (life/lifetime). Night of weeping vs morning of joy. Wrath is momentary; favor is lasting.

    ISA 54:7¶

    Notes: Small moment (rega qaton) of forsaking vs great mercies (rachamim gedolim). Explicit temporal asymmetry.

    ISA 54:8¶

    Notes: Little wrath (shetseph qetseph) vs everlasting kindness (chesed olam). The wrath-to-mercy ratio is explicitly 'a moment' vs 'everlasting'.

    JOB 34:11¶

    Notes: Proportionality: render according to ways. Elihu's statement about divine justice.

    JOB 34:12¶

    Notes: God will not do wickedly or pervert mishpat. Divine justice is inherently equitable.

    GEN 18:25¶

    Notes: Abraham appeals TO God's justice character as the basis of his argument. The premise is that God's justice is so certain that Abraham can argue from it. Shaphat kol ha-arets = Judge of all the earth. Mishpat = do right/justice.

    EZE 18:23¶

    Notes: Rhetorical question: God has NO pleasure (chaphets) in the death of the wicked. His desire is repentance and life.

    EZE 18:32¶

    Notes: Repeated: no pleasure in death. The verb is chaphets — to delight in, take pleasure in. Negated.

    EZE 33:11¶

    Notes: OATH-LEVEL statement: 'As I live' — the strongest possible divine affirmation. God swears by His own life that He has no pleasure in the death of the wicked.

    EZE 18:4¶

    Notes: The soul (nephesh) that sins shall die (muth). The stated penalty is death, not torment.

    EZE 18:20¶

    Notes: Repeated: soul that sins shall die. Also establishes individual accountability — each bears their own.

    GEN 2:17¶

    Notes: The original penalty announcement: mot tamut (dying thou shalt die). No mention of eternal torment. The penalty for sin as stated by God Himself is death.

    GEN 3:19¶

    Notes: The penalty enacted: dissolution back to dust. Not eternal conscious suffering but reversal of creation.

    ISA 28:21¶

    Notes: Judgment is God's 'strange act' (zar maaseh) and 'strange work' (nokriyyah abodah). Zar = alien, foreign, belonging to another. Nokriyyah = foreign, alien. Judgment is alien to God's character — it is not His natural work.

    1CO 15:26¶

    Notes: Death (thanatos) is the LAST enemy to be destroyed (katargeo = abolished, rendered inoperative). If ECT is true, death still exists eternally.

    PSA 145:8¶

    Notes: Echo of Exo 34:6. Channun + rachum + erek appayim + gedol-chesed.

    PSA 145:9¶

    Notes: Universal scope: towb YHWH la-kol (good to ALL). Rachamav al kol maasav (tender mercies over ALL his works). The double 'kol' (all) emphasizes universality.

    ROM 2:5¶

    Notes: Dikaiokrisia (G1341) — righteous judgment. Compound: dikaios + krisis. Hapax. God's judgment is inherently RIGHTEOUS. The day reveals the CHARACTER of God's judgment.

    ROM 2:6¶

    Notes: Apodidomi (render/repay) + kata ta erga (according to the works). Proportionality principle.

    ROM 2:7¶

    Notes: Believers SEEK (zeteo, present active participle) aphtharsia (immortality/incorruptibility). Seeking implies non-possession. Embedded anthropological prerequisite within the judgment passage.

    ROM 2:5-11¶

    Notes: Complete passage: dikaiokrisia (v.5) + kata ta erga (v.6) + seek immortality (v.7) + no respect of persons (v.11). Proportionality + anthropological prerequisite + impartiality.

    LUK 12:47¶

    Notes: Many stripes for the one who KNEW. Punishment graduated by knowledge.

    LUK 12:48¶

    Notes: Few stripes for the one who did NOT know. Proportionality is intrinsic to God's justice. This is Jesus' own teaching.

    MAT 10:15¶

    Notes: More tolerable (anektoteron) — degrees of judgment. Jesus teaches proportional judgment.

    MAT 11:22¶

    Notes: Repeated proportionality principle. More tolerable for Tyre/Sidon than Chorazin/Bethsaida.

    MAT 11:24¶

    Notes: Third instance of 'more tolerable' — proportionality confirmed across multiple sayings.

    HEB 2:2¶

    Notes: Misthapodosia (G3405) = just recompense. Endikon = within right/lawful. EVERY transgression received proportional recompense. Proportionality built into the vocabulary itself.

    HEB 10:29¶

    Notes: Timoria (G5098) = vindictive punishment. 'Sorer punishment' (cheiron timoria) — degrees of punishment exist. Qal wahomer (lesser to greater) argument. Punishment is proportional to the covenant violated.

    COL 3:25¶

    Notes: Punishment matches the specific wrong. 'For the wrong which he hath done' — not an infinite penalty for a finite wrong.

    REV 20:12¶

    Notes: Kata ta erga (according to their works). Final judgment is proportional. Books opened = individual record.

    REV 20:13¶

    Notes: Repeated: judged kata ta erga. Even in Revelation's final judgment, proportionality governs.

    2PE 3:9¶

    Notes: God is NOT willing (boulomai) that any should perish (apollymi). Universal salvific desire. Makrothumeo = longsuffering/patient.

    1TI 2:4¶

    Notes: God WILLS (thelo) all men to be saved. Universal salvific will.

    JHN 3:16¶

    Notes: The motive is LOVE (agapao). The alternative is perish (apollymi) vs. eternal life (zoe aionios). The binary is perish/life, not torment/life.

    JHN 3:17¶

    Notes: God's purpose in sending Christ is SALVATION, not condemnation.

    ROM 6:23¶

    Notes: The stated penalty is DEATH (thanatos), not torment. Wages (opsonia) = military pay = proportional compensation. Gift (charisma) = eternal life. The binary: death vs life.

    REV 21:4¶

    Notes: NO MORE death, sorrow, crying, pain. If ECT is true, death and sorrow exist eternally somewhere. This promise would be false.

    REV 15:3¶

    Notes: The redeemed AFFIRM God's justice AFTER seeing judgment. Dikaiai (just, G1342 fem pl) + alethinai (true, G228 fem pl). God's ways vindicated.

    REV 15:4¶

    Notes: Dikaiomata (righteous decrees/requirements, G1345) made manifest (phaneroo). All nations worship. God's judgments vindicated universally.

    ROM 3:25¶

    Notes: Endeixis (G1732) = demonstration/proof of dikaiosyne. Paresis (G3929) = temporary passing over (NOT aphesis/forgiveness). God DEMONSTRATES His righteousness at the cross. Anoche (G463) = forbearance.

    ROM 3:26¶

    Notes: The cross reveals God as BOTH dikaion (just) AND dikaiouinta (justifier). Justice and mercy meet at the cross. eis to einai auton dikaion kai dikaiouinta.

    ROM 3:25-26¶

    Notes: Combined. The cross is the DEMONSTRATION of God's justice and mercy united. Key terms: hilasterion (propitiation), endeixis (demonstration), dikaiosyne (righteousness), paresis (passing over), anoche (forbearance), dikaios kai dikaioun (just and justifier).

    2PE 2:6¶

    Notes: Sodom reduced to ASHES (tephroo, G5077 — to turn to ashes). Made an EXAMPLE (hupodeigma, G5262 — pattern/paradigm). The paradigmatic judgment is ashes/destruction, not ongoing torment.

    MAT 25:46¶

    Notes: Kolasis aionios. Kolasis (G2851) = punishment (from kolazo, to curtail), NOT basanizo (torment). Aionios = pertaining to the age. See word study: kolasis is judicial penalty, not torment.

    2TH 1:9¶

    Notes: Olethros aionios = everlasting DESTRUCTION (not torment). Paul chose olethros (destruction), not basanizo (torment). This is the explicit didactic definition of eschatological punishment.

    ROM 5:8¶

    Notes: God demonstrates (synistemi) His love. Christ DIED (apothnesko) — the penalty Christ bore was death, not eternal torment.

    MAT 10:28¶

    Notes: God is able to DESTROY (apollymi — active voice) both soul and body in Gehenna. The verb is destroy, not torment.

    HEB 10:26-31¶

    Notes: Fiery indignation shall DEVOUR (esthio = eat/consume) the adversaries (v.27). The fire devours — consumptive language. Proportionality: sorer punishment for greater covenant violation (v.29).

    1JN 4:8¶

    Notes: God IS love (ho theos agape estin). Love is not merely an attribute — it is God's essential nature.

    1JN 4:9¶

    Notes: Love manifested in giving — purpose is LIFE (zao), not punishment.

    1JN 4:10¶

    Notes: Love defined by initiative: God loved first. Hilasmos = propitiation/atonement.

    ROM 8:32¶

    Notes: God's generosity: did not spare His own Son — a fortiori argument for God's giving nature.

    REV 16:5¶

    Notes: Angel affirms God's righteousness IN His judgments. Dikaios ei = thou art righteous.

    REV 16:7¶

    Notes: Altar confirms: true (alethinai) and righteous (dikaiai) are thy judgments (kriseis).

    REV 19:1¶

    Notes: Praise follows judgment execution. Heaven affirms God's acts.

    REV 19:2¶

    Notes: Repeated: true and righteous judgments. God's justice affirmed AFTER execution.

    PSA 89:14¶

    Notes: Tsedeq and mishpat are the FOUNDATION of God's throne. Chesed and emeth go before His face. Justice and mercy are not in conflict — they are both foundational.

    DEU 32:4¶

    Notes: Perfect (tamim) work. All ways are mishpat. Emunah (faithfulness), no avel (injustice), tsaddiq and yashar. Comprehensive character declaration.

    ISA 6:1-5¶

    Notes: Triple qadosh (holy, holy, holy). Holiness inspires awe but the result is Isaiah's cleansing (v.6-7), not eternal torment. Holiness leads to purification, not endless punishment.

    HAB 1:13¶

    Notes: God's purity — cannot look on iniquity. This establishes holiness but says nothing about infinite punishment. Habakkuk's complaint is about God's RESTRAINT in not punishing the wicked sooner.

    LEV 10:1-2¶

    Notes: Holiness violation resulted in DEATH (muth), not eternal torment. Fire devoured (akal) and they died. The judgment for violating holiness is death.

    1SA 6:19¶

    Notes: Holiness violation: smote (nakah) = death/slaughter. Not eternal torment. The result of violating God's holiness is consistently death in OT narratives.

    REV 22:12¶

    Notes: Misthos (G3408) = reward/wages. Kata to ergon = according to his work. Proportionality in final judgment — the last chapter of the Bible.

    MAT 16:27¶

    Notes: Jesus teaches proportional recompense: kata ten praxin autou = according to his practice/deeds.

    GEN 19:24-25¶

    Notes: The paradigmatic divine judgment: Sodom overthrown (haphak = overthrow, total destruction). Result is ashes (2 Pet 2:6), not ongoing torment.

    JUD 1:7¶

    Notes: Sodom: 'example' (deigma) of 'eternal fire' (aionios pur). But 2 Pet 2:6 says the result is ASHES. The fire is 'eternal' in its effect (permanent destruction), not in duration of burning.

    DEU 25:1-3¶
