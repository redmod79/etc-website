    Word Studies¶

    Question¶

    God's love, justice, holiness, mercy, wrath. Proportionality of punishment. Consistency of infinite torment vs. destruction with biblical justice.

    H2617 — checed¶

    Occurrences: 255

    Relevance: Core divine character attribute in Exo 34:6-7. Extends to 'thousands' — mercy vastly outweighs judgment in God's self-revelation.

    H639 — aph¶

    Occurrences: 305

    Relevance: Part of erek appayim (slow to anger = 'long of nostrils') — God's patience as delayed anger. Part of divine name formula.

    H4941 — mishpat¶

    Occurrences: 448

    Relevance: Foundation of God's throne (Psa 89:14). Core justice term. God's judgment is inherently just, not arbitrary.

    H6664 — tsedeq¶

    Occurrences: 119

    Relevance: Foundation of God's throne with mishpat (Psa 89:14, 97:2). The objective norm of rightness against which all judgment is measured.

    H6666 — tsedaqah¶

    Occurrences: 165

    Relevance: God's saving justice — His righteousness IS His saving intervention. Paired with mishpat 46x as comprehensive justice.

    G1342 — dikaios¶

    Occurrences: 81

    Relevance: God described as dikaios in Rev 16:5; Christ as 'the Just One' (Acts 3:14). Used in Rev 15:3 — 'just and true are thy ways.' The redeemed affirm God's justice.

    G1343 — dikaiosyne¶

    Occurrences: 92

    Relevance: God's righteousness demonstrated (endeixis) at the cross (Rom 3:25-26). Both just and justifier.

    G2851 — kolasis¶

    Occurrences: 2

    Relevance: Matt 25:46 — 'everlasting punishment.' Kolasis is judicial penalty (from 'to curtail'), NOT torment (basanizo). The word chosen is about cutting off, not endless suffering.

    G5098 — timoria¶

    Occurrences: 1

    Relevance: Heb 10:29 — 'sorer punishment.' Degrees of punishment exist (cheiron timoria). Proportionality is built into the vocabulary.

    G3709 — orge¶

    Occurrences: 36

    Relevance: Divine wrath is judicial (settled displeasure), not emotional fury. Believers are delivered FROM wrath (1 Thess 1:10, 5:9). God has not appointed us to wrath.

    G2372 — thumos¶

    Occurrences: 18

    Relevance: 56% of uses in Revelation. Describes divine eschatological fury. When paired with orge (6x), thumos is the passionate intensity while orge is the settled state.

    H2534 — chemah¶

    Occurrences: 133

    Relevance: God's fury is 'poured out' (shaphak) and 'accomplished' (kalah) — it has a beginning and END. Lam 4:11 — 'The LORD hath accomplished his fury.' Fury that is accomplished is not perpetual.

    G622 — apollymi¶

    Occurrences: 102

    Relevance: The standard NT word for the fate of the wicked: 'should not perish but have everlasting life' (John 3:16). The perish/life binary. God able to 'destroy both soul and body' (Matt 10:28).

    G3639 — olethros¶

    Occurrences: 4

    Relevance: 2 Thess 1:9 — 'everlasting DESTRUCTION' (not torment). Paul's deliberate word choice for eschatological punishment. All 4 uses are Pauline.

    G684 — apoleia¶

    Occurrences: 20

    Relevance: The eschatological destiny word: 'broad way that leads to destruction' (Matt 7:13). Contrasted with salvation/life. Names the state, not ongoing process.

    G2288 — thanatos¶

    Occurrences: 119

    Relevance: The STATED penalty: 'wages of sin is death' (Rom 6:23). The 'last enemy destroyed' (1 Cor 15:26). 'No more death' (Rev 21:4). Death is abolished, not perpetuated.

    G1341 — dikaiokrisia¶

    Occurrences: 1

    Relevance: Rom 2:5 — the compound word itself encodes that God's judgment is inherently RIGHTEOUS. The day of wrath is also the day of RIGHTEOUS judgment.

    G3405 — misthapodosia¶

    Occurrences: 3

    Relevance: Heb 2:2 — 'every transgression received a just recompense.' The word itself is a compound meaning 'wages-giving-back' — proportionality built into the vocabulary. Also Heb 10:35, 11:26.

    G25 — agapao¶

    Occurrences: 147

    Relevance: John 3:16 — 'God so loved (agapao) the world.' The verb describing God's fundamental disposition toward humanity.

    G26 — agape¶

    Occurrences: 116

    Relevance: 'God IS love' (1 John 4:8, 16). Love is God's essential nature. Agape defines who God IS, not just what He does.

    G166 — aionios¶

    Occurrences: 71

    Relevance: Matt 25:46 — 'everlasting punishment'; 2 Thess 1:9 — 'everlasting destruction.' Hebrews pattern: aionios + result-noun = permanent result of completed act. 'Eternal punishment' = punishment with permanent results.

    G463 — anoche¶

    Occurrences: 2

    Relevance: Rom 2:4 and 3:25-26. God's forbearance = self-restraint in withholding judgment, purposed toward repentance. God restrains His own justice to give time for repentance.

    G1732 — endeixis¶

    Occurrences: 4

    Relevance: Rom 3:25-26 — the cross is the DEMONSTRATION (endeixis) of God's righteousness. God's character is SHOWN, not merely claimed.

    G3929 — paresis¶

    Occurrences: 1

    Relevance: Rom 3:25 — God's temporary passing over of sins in forbearance. Distinguished from full forgiveness. The cross resolves what forbearance merely delayed.

    G3408 — misthos¶

    Occurrences: 29

    Relevance: Rev 22:12 — 'my reward is with me, to give every man according as his work shall be.' The wages/payment framework implies proportionality. Connected to Rom 6:23 (opsonia = military wages).

    H6918 — qadowsh¶

    Occurrences: 119

    Relevance: God's holiness (Isa 6:3 triple qadosh). Holiness means set apart/distinct, not 'demands infinite punishment.' Every recorded holiness violation in OT resulted in death, not eternal torment.

    G5485 — charis¶

    Occurrences: 156

    Relevance: God's unmerited favor. The Spirit of GRACE (Heb 10:29) — despising grace increases culpability. Grace is God's default posture.

    H5162 — nacham¶

    Occurrences: 122

    Relevance: God relents (Niphal) from judgment: Joel 2:13, Jonah 3:10, Jer 18:8. God's willingness to RELENT from declared judgment reveals that punishment is not His fixed desire.

    G928 — basanizo¶

    Occurrences: 12

    Relevance: NEVER applied to human eschatological fate in didactic passages. Torment vocabulary for humans = zero epistles, zero Gospel teachings, zero OT. Used for demons (Matt 8:29), rich man in parable (Luke 16:23), and Revelation's apocalyptic imagery.

    H157 — ahab¶

    Occurrences: 225

    Relevance: OT divine love verb. God's love for Israel (Deut 7:8, Hos 11:1). The OT foundation for NT agapao/agape.

    G1349 — dike¶

    Occurrences: 4

    Relevance: Jude 1:7 — Sodom suffering 'vengeance (dike) of eternal fire.' But 2 Pet 2:6 says result = ashes. 2 Thess 1:9 — punished with destruction.

    H5359 — naqam¶

    Occurrences: 17

    Relevance: Deut 32:35 — 'Vengeance belongeth unto me.' Divine vengeance is proportional: 'as thou hast done, it shall be done unto thee' (Oba 1:15).

    H2603 — chanan¶

    Occurrences: 85

    Relevance: Part of divine name formula (Exo 34:6). God stoops in kindness — the root posture of grace.

    H6662 — tsaddiyq¶

    Occurrences: 210

    Relevance: Righteous/just - key character attribute of God
