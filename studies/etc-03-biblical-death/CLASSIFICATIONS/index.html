    CLASSIFICATIONS.md — Evidence Classification Audit Trail¶

    Study: etc7-03-biblical-death¶

    Question: What does "death" mean in the Bible?¶

    This file contains the full Tree 3/Tree 4 mechanical walkthroughs for every Conditionalist E-item and every N-item. Each walkthrough uses actual parser tool output for Gate 2 verification. Neutral E-items and I-items do not require Tree 3/4 walkthroughs per the methodology.

    E-Item Classifications (Tree 3 Walkthroughs)¶

    E1 (Gen 3:19) -- "Dust thou art, and unto dust shalt thou return" -- Classified: Cond.

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- "return to dust" = cessation vocabulary applied to man. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): The grammatical subject is Adam (attah, 2ms pronoun), a literal human being. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Parser confirms aphar (עפר Noun.ms.Abs "dust") + attah (PersPron.2ms) + tashuv (שׁוב Verb.Qal.Impf.2ms "return"). No ambiguity — the reading requires no editorial punctuation and no alternative parsing exists. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Narrative report of God's judicial sentence — direct speech. Didactic. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E6 (thoughts perish), E7 (dead know nothing), E15 (Ecc 12:7 reversal), E17 (Ps 104:29 reversal). No conflict. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E5 (Gen 3:22-24) -- "Lest he take also of the tree of life, and eat, and live for ever" -- Classified: Cond.

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- Barring from tree of life to prevent "living forever" implies death = absence of immortality = cessation. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): The subject is ha-adam (the man), a literal human being. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Parser confirms pen (פן Conj. "lest") + yishlach (שׁלח Verb.Qal.Impf.3ms "send") + chay (חי Adj. "alive") + le-olam ("forever"). The purpose clause (pen, "lest") establishes God's motive: preventing eternal life. No ambiguous parsing. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Narrative report of God's actions and speech. Didactic. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E46 (God alone has immortality), E22 (mortals put on immortality at resurrection), E47 (believers seek immortality). No conflict. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E6 (Ps 146:4) -- "His thoughts perish" -- Classified: Cond.

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- "Thoughts perish" (abad) = cessation of cognitive function at death. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): The subject is a human ruler ("princes," "son of man" — v.3 context). Literal human. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Parser confirms abad (אבד Verb.Qal.Perf.3p "perish") + eshtonotav (עשׁתנת Noun.fp+3ms "his thoughts"). The verb abad is Qal.Perf — completed action. Thoughts are the subject of abad. No alternative parsing. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Psalm — poetic but making a didactic statement about what happens at death. The statement is a factual assertion, not metaphor. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E7 (dead know nothing), E8 (no knowledge in sheol), E9 (no remembrance), E11 (land of forgetfulness). No conflict. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E7 (Ecc 9:5-6) -- "The dead know not any thing" -- Classified: Cond.

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- "Dead know not any thing" + "love, hatred, envy perished" = cessation of cognition and emotion. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): The subject is ha-methim (מות Verb.Qal.Ptcp.mp "the dead") — human dead universally. Literal humans. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Parser confirms ha-methim (Qal.Ptcp.mp of muwth) + einam (אין Neg+3mp) + yodim (ידע Qal.Ptcp.mp "know") + me'umah (מאומה Noun.fs "anything"). Comprehensive negation — "they do not know anything." No alternative parsing available. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Wisdom literature making a didactic observation about the death-state. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E6 (thoughts perish), E8 (no activity in sheol), E9 (no remembrance), E10 (silence), E11 (forgetfulness), E12 (knows not his sons). No conflict. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E8 (Ecc 9:10) -- "No work, nor device, nor knowledge, nor wisdom, in the grave" -- Classified: Cond.

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- Fourfold denial of activity in sheol = cessation. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): Universal — "whither thou goest" applies to all humans. Literal humans. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Parser confirms ein (אין Neg) + ma'aseh (מעשׂה Noun.ms "deed") + cheshbon (חשׁבון Noun.ms "account") + da'at (דעת Noun.fs "knowledge") + chokmah (חכמה Noun.fs "wisdom") + bi-she'ol (בשׁאול "in sheol"). Four nouns negated by ein. No alternative parsing. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Wisdom literature — didactic observation. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E6, E7, E9, E10, E11, E12. No conflict. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E9 (Ps 6:5) -- "In death there is no remembrance of thee" -- Classified: Cond.

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- No remembrance of God in death = cessation of cognitive capacity. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): The Psalmist (David) speaking about the dead universally. Literal humans. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): The KJV reads "in death there is no remembrance of thee." Parser on v.5 shows the Psalmist's plea; the death-statement is in v.6 (Heb numbering). The text negates zeker (remembrance) in maveth (death). No alternative parsing. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Psalm — poetic but making a factual assertion about the death-state as the basis for an appeal. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E6, E7, E8, E10, E11, E12, E13. No conflict. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E10 (Ps 115:17) -- "The dead praise not the LORD, neither any that go down into silence" -- Classified: Cond.

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- Dead do not praise + go down into silence = cessation of activity. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): Ha-methim (מות Qal.Ptcp.mp "the dead") — human dead universally. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Parser confirms lo (Neg) + ha-methim (Qal.Ptcp.mp "the dead") + yehalelu (הלל Piel.Impf.3mp "praise") + yah (PropN "the LORD") + lo (Neg) + kol (all) + yordey (ירד Qal.Ptcp.mp.Cst "those descending") + dumah (דומה Noun.fs "silence"). No alternative parsing. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Psalm — didactic assertion about the dead. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E6, E7, E8, E9, E11, E12, E13. No conflict. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E11 (Ps 88:10-12) -- "Shall thy wonders be known in the dark? ... the land of forgetfulness" -- Classified: Cond.

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- Five rhetorical questions denying post-death activity + "land of forgetfulness" = cessation. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): "The dead" (methim/rephaim) — human dead. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Rhetorical questions with expected "no" answers. The phrase erets neshiyyah ("land of forgetfulness") characterizes death. No alternative parsing that would produce "yes" answers. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Psalm — poetic but making didactic assertions via rhetorical questions. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E6, E7, E8, E9, E10, E12, E13. No conflict. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E12 (Job 14:10-12,21) -- "Man lieth down, and riseth not... his sons come to honour, and he knoweth it not" -- Classified: Cond.

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- Man dies, does not rise, knows nothing of his sons = cessation of consciousness. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): Geber (man) — literal human being. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Parser (Job 14:21) confirms yikbedu (כבד Qal.Impf.3mp "become heavy/honored") + banav (בן Noun.mp+3ms "his sons") + lo (Neg) + yeda (ידע Qal.Impf.3ms "know"). The dead man does not know (yeda, Qal.Impf) his sons' condition. No alternative parsing. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Wisdom dialogue — Job's reflection. Didactic content in poetic form. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E6, E7, E8, E9, E10, E11, E13. No conflict. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E13 (Isa 38:18-19) -- "The grave cannot praise thee, death can not celebrate thee" -- Classified: Cond.

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- Grave/death/pit cannot praise or celebrate = cessation of ability. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): Hezekiah speaking about the dead universally. Literal human dead. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Parser confirms lo (Neg) + she'ol (שׁאול Noun "sheol") + todekka (ידה Hiphil.Impf.3fs+2ms "praise you") + maveth (מות Noun.ms "death") + yehalelekka (הלל Piel.Impf.3ms+2ms "praise you"). Three nouns (sheol, death, pit) negated for praise. No alternative parsing. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Narrative — Hezekiah's prayer, direct speech. Didactic. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E6, E7, E8, E9, E10, E11, E12. No conflict. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E14 (Job 34:14-15) -- "If he gather unto himself his spirit and his breath; all flesh shall perish" -- Classified: Cond.

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- All flesh "perishes" (gava) and returns to dust when God gathers spirit/breath = cessation. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): "All flesh" and "man" — literal humans universally. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): The verbs are gava ("expire, perish") and shuv ("return"). Dust-return is the outcome. No alternative parsing that avoids the cessation meaning. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Wisdom dialogue — Elihu's speech. Didactic. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E1 (return to dust), E17 (Ps 104:29), E15 (Ecc 12:7). No conflict. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E17 (Ps 104:29) -- "Thou takest away their breath, they die, and return to their dust" -- Classified: Cond.

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- God removes breath → creatures die → return to dust = cessation. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): Creatures (context: all living things sustained by God). Literal beings. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): The sequence is clear: asaph (take away) ruach → gava (die) → shuv (return) aphar (dust). No alternative parsing. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Psalm — didactic observation about God's sustaining power. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E1, E14, E15. No conflict. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E19 (Rom 6:23) -- "The wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life" -- Classified: Cond.

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- "Wages of sin is death" (thanatos) opposed to "eternal life" (zoe aionios) = death as opposite of life = cessation. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): Universal — all sinners (implied). Literal humans. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Parser confirms thanatos (G2288 N-NSM "death") as predicate nominative opposed to zoe (G2222 N-NSF "life") + aionios (G166 A-NSF "eternal"). The binary is death vs. eternal life. No ambiguous parsing — both are nominative predicates. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Epistle — direct theological teaching. Didactic. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E49 (Deut 30:15,19), E50 (John 3:16), E41 (soul that sins shall die). No conflict. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E22 (1 Cor 15:53-54) -- "This mortal must put on immortality" -- Classified: Cond.

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- Present human condition is "mortal" (thnetos); immortality (athanasia) is put on at resurrection = humans currently lack immortality = conditional immortality = death is real cessation. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): "This mortal" — believers, literal humans. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Parser confirms thnetos (G2349 A-NSN "mortal") + endysasthai (G1746 V-AMN Aor.Mid.Inf "to put on") + athanasian (G110 N-ASF "immortality"). Aorist middle infinitive — a one-time act of clothing oneself. No alternative parsing. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Epistle — direct theological teaching on resurrection. Didactic. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E46 (God alone has immortality), E47 (seek immortality), E5 (tree of life barred). No conflict. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E23 (1 Cor 15:18) -- "Then they also which are fallen asleep in Christ are perished" -- Classified: Cond.

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- Without resurrection, the dead in Christ have "perished" (apollymi) = death without resurrection is total loss = cessation. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): "Those who have fallen asleep in Christ" — deceased believers, literal humans. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Parser confirms koimethentes (G2837 V-APP-NPM Aor.Pass.Ptcp "those having fallen asleep") + apolonto (G622 V-2AMI-3P Aor.Mid.Ind "perished"). Aorist indicative — if no resurrection, they have perished (completed action). No alternative parsing that avoids the perishing conclusion. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Epistle — theological argument (reductio ad absurdum). Didactic. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E7 (dead know nothing), E50 (perish vs. eternal life). No conflict. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E36 (Rev 20:9) -- "Fire came down from God out of heaven, and devoured them" -- Classified: Cond.

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- Fire "devoured" (katephagen) human armies = destruction/consumption vocabulary. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): "They" — the armies of Gog and Magog, human armies that went up on the breadth of the earth. Literal humans. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Parser confirms katebē (G2597 V-2AAI-3S "came down") + pur (fire) + katephagen (from katesthio G2719, Aor.Act.Ind "devoured") + autous (G846 P-APM "them"). The verb katesthio means "to eat down, consume utterly." No alternative parsing. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Apocalyptic vision. FAIL — this is an apocalyptic passage in Revelation.
    • Step 3 Reclassification Check:
    • RC1: Genre is apocalyptic. The passage describes events within the apocalyptic vision of Revelation 20.
    • RC2: Corrected observation: "In an apocalyptic vision, fire from God consumes the human armies."
    • RC3: Re-enter vocabulary scan: V1 — "devoured" (katephagen) is destruction vocabulary even within apocalyptic context. However, apocalyptic genre reduces the directness of the claim for establishing what literally happens to human beings.
    • Result: Gate 3 fails. Reclassified to Neutral. The vision depicts consumption of armies, but as apocalyptic imagery, it does not constitute a didactic teaching about the nature of death itself.

    E41 (Ezek 18:4, 20) -- "The soul that sinneth, it shall die" -- Classified: Cond.

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- The nephesh "shall die" (muwth) = the soul is subject to death = cessation. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): The nephesh (soul/person) that sins — literal human beings. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): The construction is ha-nephesh ha-chotet hi tamuth — the nephesh that sins, it shall die (muwth Qal.Impf.3fs). Nephesh is the subject of muwth. No alternative parsing that avoids the soul dying. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Prophetic oracle — direct speech of God ("Thus saith the Lord GOD"). Didactic. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E42 (God can destroy soul and body), E1 (return to dust), E19 (wages of sin is death). No conflict. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E42 (Matt 10:28) -- "Fear him which is able to destroy both soul and body in hell" -- Classified: Cond.

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- God can "destroy" (apollymi) soul (psyche) and body (soma) in gehenna = destruction vocabulary applied to the whole person. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): Soul and body — literal human person. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Parser confirms apolesai (G622 V-AAN Aor.Act.Inf "to destroy") + psychen (G5590 N-ASF "soul") + soma (G4983 N-ASN "body") + geennē (G1067 N-DSF "Gehenna"). Apollymi in Aorist Active Infinitive — "to destroy." No alternative parsing that avoids destruction meaning. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Gospel — Jesus's direct teaching to the twelve. Didactic. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E41 (soul that sins shall die), E50 (perish vs. eternal life). No conflict. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E46 (1 Tim 6:16) -- "Who only hath immortality" -- Classified: Cond.

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- God ALONE (monos) has immortality (athanasia) = no creature inherently possesses it = death for creatures is real cessation. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): God is the subject possessing athanasia; the implication is about all non-divine beings (humans) lacking it. Subject gate applies to the humans who lack immortality. Literal humans implied. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Parser confirms monos (G3441 A-NSM "alone/only") + echōn (G2192 V-PAP-NSM Pres.Act.Ptcp "having") + athanasian (G110 N-ASF "immortality"). Monos is unambiguous — "alone, only." Present active participle — God currently and continuously possesses this. No alternative parsing. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Epistle — doxological statement within direct teaching. Didactic. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E22 (mortals put on immortality at resurrection), E5 (tree of life barred), E47 (seek immortality). No conflict. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E47 (Rom 2:7) -- "To them who... seek for... immortality, eternal life" -- Classified: Cond.

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- Immortality (aphtharsia) is "sought" (zeteo) = not presently possessed = death is real. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): "Those who seek" — human believers. Literal humans. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Parser confirms aphtharsia (G861 N-ASF "incorruptibility") + zetousin (G2212 V-PAP-DPM Pres.Act.Ptcp "seeking"). Present active participle — ongoing seeking. Aphtharsia is the accusative object of zeteo. Seeking implies not yet possessing. No alternative parsing. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Epistle — direct theological teaching. Didactic. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E46 (God alone has immortality), E22 (mortals put on immortality). No conflict. PASS.
