    Classification Audit Trail — etc7-05-four-hell-words¶

    This document contains the full Tree 3/4 walkthroughs for every E-item classified Cond. or ECT and every N-item. Each walkthrough was performed using actual parser tool output for Gate 2.

    E-Item Classifications¶

    E1 (Ps 6:5) -- "In death there is no remembrance of thee; in sheol who shall give thee thanks?" -- Classified: Cond.¶

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- "no remembrance," inability to praise; cessation of consciousness applied to the dead. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): The dead (hammethim) -- literal humans who have died. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Parser output: lo (Neg) + zakar (remembrance) + sheol (nether world) + yadah (Hiphil.Impf.3fs = praise). Negation particle lo directly governs both clauses. No ambiguous parsing. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Psalm of lament -- didactic direct address to God. Not parable, not apocalyptic. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E2 (Ps 88:10-12), E3 (Ps 115:17), E4 (Ecc 9:10), E5 (Isa 38:18). No conflicting E-item. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E2 (Ps 88:10-12) -- "Shall the dead praise thee? Shall thy lovingkindness be declared in the grave? or thy faithfulness in destruction?" -- Classified: Cond.¶

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- Comprehensive negation of consciousness in sheol: no wonders, no praise, no lovingkindness, no faithfulness, darkness, forgetfulness. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): The dead (methim), rephaim (H7496, shades/dead). Literal dead humans. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Parser output: ha-interrogative + methim (Qal.Ptcp = the dead) + asah (make/do) + pele (miracle). Rhetorical questions with ha-interrogative expecting "no." Rephaim (Noun.mp.Abs = ghosts/shades) + qum (Qal.Impf.3mp = arise). Sheol paralleled with Abaddon, dark, land of forgetfulness. No ambiguous parsing. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Psalm of lament -- didactic. Not parable, not apocalyptic. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E1 (Ps 6:5), E3 (Ps 115:17), E4 (Ecc 9:10). No conflict. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E3 (Ps 115:17) -- "The dead praise not the LORD, neither any that go down into silence." -- Classified: Cond.¶

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- Dead cannot praise; sheol characterized as "silence" (dumah). Cessation of activity. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): The dead (hammethim) -- literal dead humans. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Parser output: lo (Neg) + hammethim (Art + muth Qal.Ptcp.mp = the dead) + halal (Piel.Impf.3mp = praise). Yoredey dumah (Qal.Ptcp.mp.Cst + Noun.fs = those descending to silence). Clear negation; no alternative parsing. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Psalm -- didactic contrast between living and dead. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E1, E2, E4, E5. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E4 (Ecc 9:10) -- "There is no work, nor device, nor knowledge, nor wisdom, in sheol, whither thou goest." -- Classified: Cond.¶

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- Four-fold negation of consciousness: no work (maasah), no device (cheshbown), no knowledge (daath), no wisdom (chokmah) in sheol. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): Universal ("whither thou goest") -- all humans. Literal. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Parser output: ayin (Noun.ms.Cst = NEG/nothing) + maasah (Noun.ms.Abs = deed) + cheshbown (Noun.ms.Abs = account) + daath (Noun.fs.Abs = knowledge) + chokmah (Noun.fs.Abs = wisdom) + be-sheol (Prep + Noun.s.Abs = in sheol). Negative particle ayin governs all four nouns. No alternative parsing. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Wisdom literature -- didactic. Qoheleth's direct teaching. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E1, E2, E3, E5. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E5 (Isa 38:18) -- "Sheol cannot praise thee, death cannot celebrate thee; they that go down into the pit cannot hope for thy truth." -- Classified: Cond.¶

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- Sheol/death/pit characterized by inability to praise, celebrate, or hope. Cessation of conscious activity. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): Those who go down to the pit (yoredey bor) -- literal dead. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Parser output: lo (Neg) + sheol (Noun.s.Abs = nether world) + yadah (Hiphil.Impf.3fs = praise). Maveth (Noun.ms.Abs = death) + halal (Piel.Impf.3ms = praise). Lo (Neg) + sabar (Piel.Impf.3mp = hope) + yoredey (Qal.Ptcp.mp.Cst = those descending) + bor (Noun.ms.Abs = cistern/pit). Triple negation; three parallel terms. No ambiguous parsing. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Hezekiah's thanksgiving -- narrative/poetic. Direct statement about sheol's nature. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E1-E4. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E6 (Matt 10:28) -- "Fear him which is able to destroy both soul and body in gehenna." -- Classified: Cond.¶

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- "destroy" (apollymi G622) applied to soul and body in gehenna. Destruction vocabulary. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): "Soul and body" -- literal human components. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Parser output: apolesai (apollymi G622, Aor Act Inf = to destroy) + psychen (psyche G5590, Acc Sg F = soul) + soma (soma G4983, Acc Sg N = body) + geenne (geenna G1067, Dat Sg F = gehenna). Apollymi in active voice infinitive = to destroy. No alternative parsing; the verb unambiguously means "destroy" in this construction. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Direct teaching (Jesus instructs the twelve) -- didactic. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E7 (Matt 5:29-30 -- apollymi + gehenna), E8 (Luke 12:5 -- kill then cast into gehenna). No conflicting E-item. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E7 (Matt 5:29-30) -- "It is profitable for thee that one of thy members should perish, and not that thy whole body should be cast into gehenna." -- Classified: Cond.¶

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- Member "perishes" (apollymi G622); body "cast" into gehenna. Destruction/disposal vocabulary. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): "Body" and "members" -- literal human. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Apollymi (G622) = perish/destroy. Ballo (G906) = cast. Standard grammar; no alternative parsing changes the destruction reading. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Sermon on the Mount -- didactic teaching. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E6 (Matt 10:28), E8 (Luke 12:5). PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E8 (Luke 12:5) -- "Fear him, which after he hath killed hath power to cast into gehenna." -- Classified: Cond.¶

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- Sequence: God kills (apokteinai, G615), THEN casts into gehenna. Death precedes gehenna. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): Implied human subjects (those whom God can kill and cast). PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Parser output: meta (G3326, Prep = after) + to (Art) + apokteinai (apokteino G615, Aor Act Inf = to kill) + echonta (echo G2192, Pres Act Ptcp Acc Sg M = having) + exousian (exousia G1849, Acc Sg F = authority) + embalein (emballo G1685, Aor Act Inf = to cast into) + geennan (geenna G1067, Acc Sg F). The temporal preposition meta + articular infinitive = "after killing." Temporal sequence is grammatically unambiguous. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Direct teaching (Jesus warns disciples). Didactic. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E6 (Matt 10:28), E7. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    E9 (Hos 13:14) -- "O death, I will be thy plagues; O sheol, I will be thy destruction." -- Classified: Cond.¶

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- God DESTROYS death and sheol. Destruction vocabulary applied to sheol itself. Candidate: COND.
