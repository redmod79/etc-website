    Word Studies¶

    Question¶

    Semantic range of Sheol (H7585), Hades (G86), Gehenna (G1067), Tartaroo (G5020). Interchangeability? Does any inherently mean eternal torment? LXX translation patterns.

    H7585 — sheol¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/H7585-showl.md

    Occurrences: 68

    The realm of the dead. 64 verses, 68 tokens. KJV: 'hell' 31x, 'grave' 26x, 'pit' 3x. LXX: hades 58x (PMI 8.31). Receives both righteous and wicked. Characterized by unconsciousness in didactic passages. Paired with death (maveth), Abaddon, pit.

    Semantic categories: realm of the dead, descent/below, death parallelism, wicked fate, rescue from sheol, insatiability

    Key verses: Gen 37:35, Ps 6:5, Ps 16:10, Ecc 9:10, Hos 13:14

    Relevance: Primary study word — Hebrew term behind the 'hell' question.

    G86 — hades¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G86-háidēs.md

    Occurrences: 11

    NT equivalent of sheol. 11 occurrences. LXX: sheol to hades 58x. Categories: realm of the dead (Acts 2:27,31), judgment (Matt 11:23), defeated power (Rev 1:18; 20:13-14). Temporary — cast into lake of fire. KJV: 'hell' 10x, 'grave' 1x.

    Semantic categories: realm of the dead, judgment/humiliation, defeated power

    Key verses: Acts 2:27, Acts 2:31, Rev 20:13-14, 1 Cor 15:55, Luke 16:23

    Relevance: Second study word — Greek equivalent of sheol confirmed by Acts 2:27,31.

    G1067 — gehenna¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G1067-geenna.md

    Occurrences: 12

    Transliteration of ge-Hinnom. 12 NT occurrences. Jesus 11x, James 1x. Collocates: fire 6x, apollymi 3x. Contrast: 'enter life' vs. gehenna. Matt 10:28 uses apollymi (destroy), NOT basanizo (torment). No gehenna passage applies torment vocabulary to humans.

    Semantic categories: fiery destruction, self-denial imperative, soul/body destruction, divine judgment

    Key verses: Matt 5:22, Matt 10:28, Mark 9:43-48, Luke 12:5, James 3:6

    Relevance: Third study word — NT term most associated with 'hell' in popular understanding.

    G5020 — tartaroo¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G5020-tartaroo.md

    Occurrences: 1

    Hapax legomenon — verb, not noun. 2 Pet 2:4 only. Applied exclusively to fallen angels. 'Reserved unto judgment' = temporary imprisonment, not final punishment.

    Semantic categories: divine incarceration of angels

    Key verses: 2 Pet 2:4

    Relevance: Fourth study word — single occurrence, angel-only application.

    G622 — apollymi¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G622-apollymi.md

    Occurrences: 102

    To destroy fully, perish, lose. 86 verses, 102 tokens. Active: destroy/kill. Middle/passive: perish. Hebrew source: abad (H6), LXX 141x. Used in gehenna (Matt 10:28: 'destroy both soul and body').

    Semantic categories: physical destruction, divine judgment, eschatological perishing, losing life/soul, losing possession

    Key verses: Matt 10:28, John 3:16, Luke 17:27,29, 2 Pet 3:9

    Relevance: The verb Jesus uses in gehenna passages — 'destroy,' not 'torment.'

    G684 — apoleia¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G684-apoleia.md

    Occurrences: 20

    Destruction, perdition, waste. 20 tokens. KJV: 'destruction' 5x, 'perdition' 5x. Noun form of apollymi (G622). Broad way leading to destruction (Matt 7:13), son of perdition.

    Semantic categories: eschatological ruin, son of perdition, waste

    Key verses: Matt 7:13, John 17:12, Rev 17:8, Phil 3:19

    Relevance: Noun form of destroy/perish vocabulary.

    G929 — basanismos¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G929-basanismos.md

    Occurrences: 6

    Torment. 6 occurrences, all Revelation. Demonic torment (Rev 9:5), smoke of torment (Rev 14:11), Babylon's torment (Rev 18:7,10,15). Alludes to Isa 34:9-10.

    Semantic categories: demonic torment, eschatological judgment, commercial/imperial torment

    Key verses: Rev 14:11, Rev 9:5, Rev 18:7,10,15

    Relevance: Torment noun — Revelation-specific contexts. Smoke imagery = OT destruction-completion formula.

    G2851 — kolasis¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G2851-kolasis.md

    Occurrences: 2

    Punishment, penal infliction. Only 2 NT occurrences: Matt 25:46, 1 John 4:18. Root kolazo = curtail/prune — corrective punishment.

    Semantic categories: eschatological punishment, fear/anguish

    Key verses: Matt 25:46, 1 John 4:18

    Relevance: Key word in Matt 25:46. Etymology: curtailment, not torture.

    G166 — aionios¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G166-aionios.md

    Occurrences: 69

    Adjective from aion (G165). 71 tokens. KJV: 'eternal' 32x, 'everlasting' 21x. Dominant: 'eternal life' (~43 of 69). Also modifies fire, punishment, destruction, chains. Hebrew source: olam (H5769), 100x.

    Semantic categories: eternal life, eternal judgment, divine attributes, eternal realities

    Key verses: John 3:16, Matt 25:46, 2 Thess 1:9, Jude 1:7, Rom 6:23

    Relevance: Modifies both positive (life) and negative (punishment, destruction, fire) outcomes.

    G126 — aidios¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G126-aidios.md

    Occurrences: 2

    Everduring, eternal. 2 NT occurrences: Rom 1:20 (God's eternal power), Jude 1:6 (everlasting chains). From aei (always). Absolute eternality.

    Semantic categories: divine attributes, eschatological judgment

    Key verses: Rom 1:20, Jude 1:6

    Relevance: Used for chains binding fallen angels — absolute permanence, but 'unto judgment' (temporary holding).

    G762 — asbestos¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G762-ásbestos.md

    Occurrences: 4

    Unquenchable. 4 NT occurrences: Matt 3:12, Mark 9:43, Mark 9:45, Luke 3:17. All modify fire. 'Unquenchable' = cannot be extinguished before completing its work.

    Semantic categories: eschatological judgment fire

    Key verses: Mark 9:43, Mark 9:45, Matt 3:12, Luke 3:17

    Relevance: 'Unquenchable' = fire completing its work. Chaff is totally consumed (Matt 3:12).

    G4663 — skolex¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G4663-skṓlēx.md

    Occurrences: 3

    Worm, maggot. 3 NT occurrences (Mark 9:44,46,48), quoting Isa 66:24. Decomposition agent feeding on corpses. Hebrew source: tolaath (H8438).

    Semantic categories: decay of unburied dead

    Key verses: Mark 9:44, Mark 9:46, Mark 9:48

    Relevance: Worm = decomposition agent on CORPSES (peger), not living souls.

    H8438 — tolaath¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/H8438-tôwlâʻ.md

    Occurrences: 44

    Maggot; also crimson-grub dye. 44 occurrences. Dominant: scarlet dye (70%+). Literal worm: Isa 14:11, 66:24. LXX: skolex (G4663) 7x.

    Semantic categories: scarlet dye, purification rituals, literal worm/decay, metaphor for lowliness, eschatological judgment

    Key verses: Isa 66:24, Isa 14:11, Psa 22:6

    Relevance: Hebrew behind Isa 66:24's worm (Mark 9:48). Decomposition agent.

    H11 — abaddon¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/H11-ʼabaddown.md

    Occurrences: 5

    Destruction — place/realm. 5 OT occurrences. Intensive of abad (H6). Always paired with sheol/death. LXX: apoleia (G684) 4x. NT Rev 9:11: Abaddon = Apollyon (Destroyer).

    Semantic categories: place of destruction, personified destruction, consuming ruin

    Key verses: Job 26:6, Psa 88:11, Pro 15:11, Rev 9:11

    Relevance: Sheol's constant companion — associates sheol with DESTRUCTION, not torment.

    H6297 — peger¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/H6297-peger.md

    Occurrences: 24

    Carcass, corpse, dead body. 24 occurrences. NEVER used for a living person. In Isa 66:24: subjects of worm and fire are dead bodies.

    Semantic categories: battlefield dead, divine judgment, prophetic imagery

    Key verses: Isa 66:24, Num 14:29, Isa 34:3

    Relevance: The word in Isa 66:24 Jesus quotes. Worm and fire consume CORPSES, not living souls.

    H1516 — gay/ge¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/H1516-gayʼ.md

    Occurrences: 67

    Valley, gorge. 67 occurrences. The ge- in ge-Hinnom (gehenna). Geographic feature with steep sides. H2011 Hinnom collocates 13x.

    Semantic categories: geographic feature, named valleys, battlefield geography

    Key verses: Jos 15:8, Jer 7:31, 2Ki 23:10

    Relevance: Hebrew noun behind gehenna's first element. Establishes gehenna as a physical place name.

    H2011 — Hinnom¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/H2011-hinnom.md

    Occurrences: 13

    Proper name (Jebusite). 13 occurrences. Trajectory: boundary (Joshua) → sacrifice site (Kings) → prophetic curse (Jeremiah) → settlement (Nehemiah).

    Semantic categories: geographic boundary, child sacrifice site, prophetic judgment, post-exilic settlement

    Key verses: Jos 15:8, 2Ki 23:10, Jer 7:31-32, Neh 11:30

    Relevance: Proper noun behind gehenna. OT trajectory: geography → abomination → slaughter → geographic ref.

    H8612 — Topheth¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/H8612-tôpheth.md

    Occurrences: 10

    Burning site in Valley of Hinnom. 10 occurrences. Where children sacrificed to Molech. Possibly from taphaph (to drum). Jeremiah: renamed 'Valley of Slaughter.'

    Semantic categories: child sacrifice site, divine judgment, cultic reform

    Key verses: Jer 7:31, 2Ki 23:10, Jer 19:6, Isa 30:33

    Relevance: Specific location of burning in ge-Hinnom. Establishes fire-association.

    H7496 — rapha/rephaim¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/H7496-râphâʼ.md

    Occurrences: 8

    Shades/dead ones in sheol. 8 occurrences. Weak, powerless shadows. From raphah (H7503, to be feeble). LXX: nekros 3x, hades 3x.

    Semantic categories: the dead in sheol, death as destination, resurrection/non-resurrection

    Key verses: Job 26:5, Isa 14:9, Isa 26:14, Psa 88:10

    Relevance: Inhabitants of sheol — characterized by WEAKNESS, not consciousness.

    H7845 — shachath (pit)¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/H7845-shachath.md

    Occurrences: 23

    Pit, corruption, destruction. 23 occurrences. From H7843 (destroy/corrupt). Sheol parallel. LXX: diaphthora (G1312) 8x. Ps 16:10 'corruption' quoted in Acts 2:27.

    Semantic categories: literal pit/trap, death/grave parallel, corruption/decay, deliverance from pit

    Key verses: Psa 16:10, Job 33:18,24, Isa 38:17

    Relevance: Sheol parallel — associates realm of dead with corruption/decay.

    G930 — basanistes¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G930-basanistēs.md

    Occurrences: 1

    Tormentor. Hapax: Matt 18:34 (parable). Agent noun from basanizo. Originally touchstone tester; then torturer/jailer.

    Semantic categories: punitive authority

    Key verses: Matt 18:34

    Relevance: Part of basanizo family. Only in a parable about earthly debt, not eschatology.

    G931 — basanos¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G931-basanos.md

    Occurrences: 3

    Touchstone; then torment. 3 occurrences: Matt 4:24 (disease), Luke 16:23,28 (hades parable). Original: testing stone for gold purity.

    Semantic categories: physical suffering, afterlife torment, testing

    Key verses: Luke 16:23, Luke 16:28, Matt 4:24

    Relevance: Torment noun in Luke 16:23 (parable). Original meaning: testing, not eternal punishment.

    G3041 — limne¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G3041-limne.md

    Occurrences: 10

    Lake. 10 occurrences: 5 Luke (Gennesaret), 5 Revelation (lake of fire). 'Lake of fire' = 'second death' (Rev 20:14; 21:8). Collocates: pyr 5x, ballo 4x, thanatos 2x.

    Semantic categories: natural body of water, eschatological place of punishment

    Key verses: Rev 19:20, Rev 20:10, Rev 20:14-15, Rev 21:8

    Relevance: Lake of fire DISTINCT from hades (hades cast INTO it). Identified as second DEATH.

    G165 — aion¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G165-aion.md

    Occurrences: 120

    Age, era, perpetuity. 95 verses, 120 tokens. From aei (always). Root of aionios. KJV: 'ever' 50x, 'world' 29x. Hebrew source: olam (H5769) 287x.

    Semantic categories: duration, age/era, universe/world

    Key verses: Rev 20:10, Heb 1:2, Matt 12:32

    Relevance: Root word of aionios. 'Eis tous aionas ton aionon' = emphatic duration formula.

    G3639 — olethros¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G3639-olethros.md

    Occurrences: 4

    Destruction, ruin. 4 Pauline occurrences. From ollymi (destroy). 2 Thess 1:9: 'everlasting destruction.' KJV consistently: 'destruction.' No lexicon defines as torment.

    Semantic categories: disciplinary destruction, sudden destruction, everlasting destruction

    Key verses: 2 Thess 1:9, 1 Thess 5:3, 1 Tim 6:9

    Relevance: Paul's word for wicked's fate. Destruction, not torment.

    G2288 — thanatos¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G2288-thanatos.md

    Occurrences: 119

    Death. 106 verses. Hebrew source: maveth (H4194) 97x LXX. 'Second death' in Rev 20:14; 21:8. Death = enemy to be destroyed (1 Cor 15:26).

    Semantic categories: physical death, second death, death as enemy, death personified

    Key verses: Rev 20:14, Rev 21:8, 1 Cor 15:26, Rom 6:23

    Relevance: Lake of fire = second DEATH. Human fate: thanatos (death), not basanizo (torment).

    G4442 — pyr¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G4442-pyr.md

    Occurrences: 73

    Fire. 73 verses. Hebrew source: esh (H784) 316x LXX. Concentrated in Revelation (25x). Collocates: gehenna 6x, limne 5x, aionios 3x.

    Semantic categories: literal fire, divine judgment fire, purification fire, theophany fire

    Key verses: Matt 5:22, Mark 9:43, Rev 20:14-15, Matt 25:41

    Relevance: Fire is dominant image in gehenna and lake of fire. Fire CONSUMES.

    H5769 — owlam¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/H5769-owlam.md

    Occurrences: 432

    Time out of mind, eternity. 406 verses. Core: horizon beyond sight. KJV: 'for ever' 49%, 'everlasting' 13%. LXX: aion 287x, aionios 100x. Applied to things that ended (Aaron's priesthood, hills).

    Semantic categories: indefinite duration, divine attributes, covenant permanence

    Key verses: Dan 12:2, Gen 3:22, Ps 90:2, Isa 34:10

    Relevance: Hebrew behind 'everlasting.' Core = 'beyond the horizon' — indefinite, not necessarily infinite.

    H784 — esh¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/H784-esh.md

    Occurrences: 400

    Fire. 348 verses. LXX: pyr 316x. In judgment contexts: fire consumes/destroys, does not preserve.

    Semantic categories: literal fire, sacrificial fire, divine theophany, judgment fire

    Key verses: Deu 32:22, Isa 66:24, Mal 4:1

    Relevance: OT fire in judgment = consumption/destruction.

    G5590 — psyche¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G5590-psyche.md

    Occurrences: 105

    Soul, life, self. ~105 occurrences. LXX: nephesh to psyche ~599x. Can be DESTROYED in gehenna (Matt 10:28).

    Semantic categories: inner self, life/living being, person, object lost/saved/destroyed

    Key verses: Matt 10:28, Matt 16:25, John 3:16

    Relevance: Matt 10:28: God destroys psyche in gehenna. Soul is not indestructible.

    H5315 — nephesh¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/H5315-nephesh.md

    Occurrences: 788

    Breathing creature, soul, life. 682 verses. Applied to animals. Can die (Ezek 18:4). Used for corpses. Never modified by 'immortal.'

    Semantic categories: living being, life force, person/self, corpse

    Key verses: Gen 2:7, Ezek 18:4, Lev 21:11

    Relevance: Soul (nephesh) is mortal and destructible. Prerequisite for Matt 10:28.

    G3709 — orge¶

    Source: D:/bible/bible-studies/word-studies/G3709-orge.md

    Occurrences: 36

    Wrath, anger. 36 occurrences. Hebrew source: aph (H639) 135x LXX. Dual domain: anger (emotion) and punishment (judicial). God's wrath results in destruction.

    Semantic categories: divine wrath, human anger, eschatological punishment

    Key verses: Rom 2:5, Rev 14:10, Matt 3:7

    G928 — basanizo¶

    Occurrences: 12

    Basanizo (G928) -- to test by touchstone, to torture, to torment. 12 total occurrences in the NT. Wide semantic range: Matt 8:6 (servant 'grievously tormented' = sick/paralyzed), Matt 8:29 (demons: 'art thou come to torment us?'), Matt 14:24 (ship 'tossed' by waves), Mark 5:7/Luke 8:28 (demons: 'torment me not'), Mark 6:48 (disciples 'toiling' in rowing), 2 Pet 2:8 (Lot 'vexed' his righteous soul), Rev 9:5 (scorpion torment), Rev 11:10 (two prophets 'tormented' the earth-dwellers), Rev 12:2 (woman 'pained' in childbirth), Rev 14:10 (tormented with fire and brimstone), Rev 20:10 (devil/beast/false prophet tormented day and night). CRITICAL: basanizo NEVER co-occurs with gehenna in any NT passage. Jesus uses apollymi (destroy) in gehenna contexts but never basanizo.

    Semantic categories: physical suffering/illness, demonic distress, natural forces (waves), moral anguish, divine judgment, torment/punishment

    Key verses: Matt 8:6, Matt 8:29, Matt 14:24, Rev 14:10, Rev 20:10

    Relevance: Central to the eternal torment question. If eternal conscious torment is the biblical teaching about the fate of the wicked, basanizo should appear in gehenna passages. It does not. The torment vocabulary in Revelation is applied to the devil, beast, and false prophet (symbolic/supernatural entities), not to ordinary human beings entering gehenna.
