    Question¶

    Condition of the dead -- conscious or unconscious? What happens between death and resurrection? Examine ALL Old Testament death-state passages (the dead know nothing, thoughts perish, no praise, silence, forgetfulness, sleep), ALL New Testament death-as-sleep passages, and ALL alleged conscious intermediate state passages (2 Cor 5:1-9, Phil 1:23, Luke 23:43, Luke 16:19-31, Rev 6:9-11, 1 Sam 28:3-20, Matt 17:1-9, 1 Pet 3:18-20). Examine the Hebrew and Greek 'sleep' vocabulary (shakab H7901, yashen H3462, koimao G2837, katheudo G2518) and 'rest/waiting' vocabulary. Investigate sheol (H7585) and hades (G86) as the abode of the dead.

    Prior Research Summary¶

    Extensive prior research exists across the etc6/etc7 series. etc6-04-state-of-the-dead catalogued 66 explicit statements (20 Cond., 46 Neutral, 0 ECT). etc7-01 (what-is-man) established that man BECAME a soul (holistic anthropology). etc7-02 (who-has-immortality) confirmed immortality is conditional, not inherent. etc7-03 (biblical-death) defined death as reversal of creation. etc6-05 (four-hell-words) analyzed sheol/hades/gehenna/tartaroo with 57 evidence items. Dedicated prior studies exist for Luke 16:19-31 (etc6-09), Rev 6:9-11 (etc6-10), 2 Cor 5:1-8, 1 Pet 3:18-20, 1 Sam 28, Stephen's prayer, Heb 12:23, and objections-to-soul-sleep. All alleged conscious intermediate state passages were classified Neutral after Tree 3 analysis in prior studies. This etc7 study should incorporate, build upon, and potentially refine all prior findings using the etc7 evidence classification methodology.

    Primary Verses (32 — full analysis required)¶

    OT Death-State: Unconsciousness and Silence: Old Testament passages explicitly describing the dead as unconscious, silent, unknowing, or inactive - Psa 146:4 — His breath goeth forth, he returns to earth; in that very day his thoughts perish - Eccl 9:5-6 — The dead know not any thing; no more reward; memory forgotten; love, hatred, envy perished - Eccl 9:10 — No work, device, knowledge, or wisdom in sheol whither thou goest - Psa 6:5 — In death there is no remembrance of thee; in sheol who shall give thee thanks? - Psa 115:17 — The dead praise not the LORD, neither any that go down into silence - Psa 88:10-12 — Wonders to the dead? Shall dead arise and praise? Lovingkindness in the grave? Faithfulness in destruction? Wonders in the dark? Righteousness in forgetfulness? - Job 14:10-14 — Man dies, wastes away; where is he? Lies down, rises not till heavens be no more; thou shalt call and I will answer - Job 14:21 — Dead know not whether sons come to honour or brought low — explicit unconsciousness - Isa 38:18-19 — Sheol cannot praise thee; death cannot celebrate thee; they that go down into the pit cannot hope for thy truth; the living shall praise thee

    OT and NT Sleep Vocabulary: Passages using shakab, yashen, koimao, or katheudo as metaphor for death - Dan 12:2 — Many that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake — resurrection from sleep-state - John 11:11-14 — Our friend Lazarus sleepeth; I go to awake him; then Jesus said plainly, Lazarus is dead - 1 Cor 15:18 — If no resurrection, then they which are fallen asleep in Christ are PERISHED — impossible if consciously in heaven

    Sheol/Hades Characterization: Passages describing the nature and character of sheol and hades - Eccl 9:10 — No work, device, knowledge, or wisdom in sheol whither thou goest - Psa 6:5 — In death there is no remembrance of thee; in sheol who shall give thee thanks? - Psa 115:17 — The dead praise not the LORD, neither any that go down into silence - Psa 88:10-12 — Wonders to the dead? Shall dead arise and praise? Lovingkindness in the grave? Faithfulness in destruction? Wonders in the dark? Righteousness in forgetfulness?

    Alleged Conscious Intermediate State Passages: Each requires detailed grammatical and contextual analysis; these are the counter-evidence to evaluate - 2 Cor 5:1-9 — Alleged conscious intermediate state — absent from body, present with Lord; earthly house dissolved - Phil 1:21-24 — Alleged conscious intermediate state — to depart and be with Christ which is far better; same letter: resurrection at 3:11,20-21 - Luke 23:43 — Alleged conscious intermediate state — 'today shalt thou be with me in paradise'; comma placement issue - Luke 16:19-31 — Alleged conscious intermediate state — rich man and Lazarus parable; Abraham's bosom - Rev 6:9-11 — Alleged conscious intermediate state — souls under the altar crying out - 1 Sam 28:3-20 — Alleged conscious intermediate state — witch of Endor; Saul and Samuel - Matt 17:1-9 — Alleged conscious intermediate state — transfiguration with Moses and Elijah; Jesus calls it 'the vision' (horama) - 1 Pet 3:18-20 — Alleged conscious intermediate state — preaching to spirits in prison

    Resurrection as the Biblical Hope: Passages presenting resurrection, not death, as the moment of receiving eternal life and being with the Lord - John 6:39-40 — I will raise him up at the last day — repeated 4x; resurrection is the hope, not immediate heaven - Dan 12:2 — Many that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake — resurrection from sleep-state - 1 Cor 15:51-54 — We shall not all sleep; mortal must put on immortality; corruptible must put on incorruption — immortality acquired at resurrection - Acts 2:29-34 — David is NOT ascended into the heavens — explicit denial of David's conscious heavenly state despite being 'a man after God's own heart' - Heb 11:39-40 — These all, having obtained a good report through faith, received NOT the promise — OT saints not yet in heaven

    Creation Anthropology and Death Reversal: The Gen 2:7 creation formula and its reversal at death - Gen 2:7 — Formation of living soul — dust + neshamah = nephesh chayyah; foundational for what 'dies' at death - Gen 3:19 — Death sentence — dust to dust; defines death as reversal of creation - Eccl 12:7 — Dust returns to earth; spirit returns to God who gave it — reversal of Gen 2:7 - Psa 146:4 — His breath goeth forth, he returns to earth; in that very day his thoughts perish

    Immortality Conditional, Not Inherent: Passages establishing that immortality belongs to God alone and is acquired at resurrection - 1 Tim 6:15-16 — God alone (monos) hath immortality — foundational for conditional immortality

    Key Cross-References for Alleged Conscious State Passages: Verses that resolve or contextualize the alleged conscious state passages - John 20:17 — I am not yet ascended to my Father — Jesus not in paradise on crucifixion day; critical for Luke 23:43 - 1 Cor 15:18 — If no resurrection, then they which are fallen asleep in Christ are PERISHED — impossible if consciously in heaven

    Additional Primary Verses: - Matt 22:31-32 — God of Abraham, Isaac, Jacob — God of the living; Jesus used to prove RESURRECTION, not present consciousness - Acts 7:59-60 — Stephen: Lord Jesus receive my spirit; he fell ASLEEP — spirit commended + sleep language combined - 1 Thess 4:13-17 — Them which are asleep; dead in Christ shall rise first; then we which are alive shall be caught up; so shall we ever be with the Lord
