    Bible Study: Lake of fire in Revelation. "Second death" (Rev 2:11; 20:6,14; 21:8). Rev 20:14 ("lake of fire IS the second death"). Death/hell cast into lake (Rev 20:14). What is the lake of fire?     Question¶

    Lake of fire in Revelation. "Second death" (Rev 2:11; 20:6,14; 21:8). Rev 20:14 ("lake of fire IS the second death"). Death/hell cast into lake (Rev 20:14). What is the lake of fire? What is the second death? What does it mean that Rev 20:14 identifies the lake of fire AS the second death? What does it mean that death and hades are cast into the lake of fire? Who are the subjects who enter the lake? What do Rev 2:11 and 20:6 say about overcomers and the second death? How does the "second death" function as the text's own explanation of what the lake does?

    Prior Research Summary¶

    etc6-13 studied the identical question in the etc6 pipeline. Key findings: (1) The identification statement (Rev 20:14) is a predicate nominative — the lake of fire IS the second death, using thanatos vocabulary, not basanizo vocabulary. (2) Five categories of subjects enter the lake with differentiated vocabulary — humans receive death/destruction vocabulary; non-human/symbolic entities receive torment vocabulary. (3) The torment formula (basanisthesontai eis tous aionas ton aionon) appears once (Rev 20:10), applied ONLY to devil/beast/false prophet — never to any human. (4) Rev 2:11 uses the strongest Greek negation (ou me + aorist passive subjunctive); Rev 20:6 uses exousia (authority/jurisdiction) language. (5) Death-of-death chain: Isa 25:8 → 1 Cor 15:26,54 → 2 Tim 1:10 → Rev 20:14 → Rev 21:4. Multiple prior studies (etc7-05, etc7-12, etc7-11, etc7-03, etc7-04, etc7-08, etc7-06) have established related findings. This etc7-13 study must re-examine all evidence with the etc7 pipeline's tool-driven verification, investigate Targum 'second death' tradition, examine the five subjects in detail, examine the X=Y self-interpreting formula pattern, and fill gaps including the gehenna/lake-of-fire relationship, Matt 25:41 ('prepared for the devil'), the 'no more death' outcome (Rev 21:4), Rev 20:9 fire that 'devoured' human armies, the book of life mechanism, and the firstborn/firstfruits connection.

    Primary Verses (33 — full analysis required)¶

    Lake of fire core passages: All five occurrences of limne tou puros plus the second death identification passages - Rev 19:20 — Beast and false prophet cast 'alive' (zontes) into lake — first subjects, symbolic entities - Rev 20:10 — Torment formula — basanisthesontai eis tous aionas ton aionon applied ONLY to devil, beast, false prophet (non-human/symbolic) - Rev 20:14-15 — Crux text — predicate nominative identification 'this is the second death, the lake of fire'; death/hades cast in; humans 'not found in book of life' cast in - Rev 21:8 — Eight categories of wicked — 'their part in the lake which burneth with fire and brimstone: which is the second death' - Rev 2:11 — Second death overcomer promise — ou me + aorist passive subjunctive, strongest Greek negation; adikeo (G91) 'not be hurt of the second death' - Rev 20:5-6 — First resurrection / second death pairing — implied second resurrection leads to second death

    Vocabulary differentiation passages: Passages showing different vocabulary for different subjects at the lake - Rev 19:20 — Beast and false prophet cast 'alive' (zontes) into lake — first subjects, symbolic entities - Rev 19:21 — Human 'remnant' slain (apektanthesan) with sword — destruction vocabulary for humans vs. 'alive' for symbolic entities in v.20 - Rev 20:9 — Fire from heaven 'devoured' (katephagen, G2719) human armies — destruction vocabulary ONE VERSE before torment formula - Rev 20:10 — Torment formula — basanisthesontai eis tous aionas ton aionon applied ONLY to devil, beast, false prophet (non-human/symbolic) - Rev 20:14-15 — Crux text — predicate nominative identification 'this is the second death, the lake of fire'; death/hades cast in; humans 'not found in book of life' cast in - Rev 21:8 — Eight categories of wicked — 'their part in the lake which burneth with fire and brimstone: which is the second death'

    Death-of-death chain: The vocabulary chain tracing death's destruction from OT promise to Revelation fulfillment - Isa 25:8 — OT foundation: 'He will swallow up death in victory' — bala (H1104) + maveth (H4194) - Hos 13:14 — OT apostrophe to death: 'O death, I will be thy plagues; O grave, I will be thy destruction' - 1 Cor 15:26 — Last enemy destroyed is death — katargeo (G2673) applied to death, parallels Rev 20:14 - 1 Cor 15:54-55 — Death swallowed up in victory — cites Isa 25:8 and Hos 13:14; nikos (G3534) over death - 2 Tim 1:10 — Christ 'hath abolished death' — katargeo (G2673), same word as 1 Cor 15:26 - Heb 2:14 — Christ partook of flesh to 'destroy' (katargeo G2673) him that had the power of death, the devil - Rev 21:4 — Outcome: 'no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain' — death itself abolished

    Fire-and-brimstone paradigm: OT fire-and-brimstone events and their interpretations showing destruction/ashes outcome - Gen 19:24-25 — OT fire-and-brimstone paradigm: Sodom/Gomorrah — fire produced destruction/ashes - Mal 4:1-3 — OT fire-and-brimstone outcome: wicked = stubble burned up, become ashes under feet - Isa 34:9-10 — Edom's smoke ascending forever — yet Isa 34:11-17 describes animals dwelling in the ruins; fire ended - 2 Pet 2:6 — Sodom 'turning to ashes' as 'an ensample unto those that after should live ungodly'

    X=Y identification formulas: Self-interpreting symbol-decoding patterns in Daniel and Revelation - Rev 1:20 — X=Y symbol-decoding formula: 'the seven stars ARE the angels... the seven candlesticks ARE the seven churches' - Rev 17:9 — X=Y self-interpreting formula: 'The seven heads ARE seven mountains' - Rev 17:15 — X=Y self-interpreting formula: 'The waters... ARE peoples, and multitudes' - Rev 17:18 — X=Y self-interpreting formula: 'the woman... IS that great city' - Rev 21:8 — Eight categories of wicked — 'their part in the lake which burneth with fire and brimstone: which is the second death'

    Book of life passages: The book of life as criterion for second death - Rev 3:5 — Overcomer promise: 'not blot out his name from book of life' — two sides of same coin with 2:11

    Additional Primary Verses: - Rev 20:6 — Second death exousia statement — 'second death hath no power (exousia)' over first resurrection participants - Rev 20:13 — Death and hades deliver up the dead — precedes their destruction in v.14 - Rev 1:18 — Christ holds keys of death and hades — authority over the entities later cast into lake - Rev 6:8 — Death and hades personified as riders — same personification as Rev 20:13-14 - Rom 6:23 — Wages of sin is death (thanatos) — same word as 'second death'; life as gift vs. death as penalty - Matt 25:41 — Eternal fire 'prepared for the devil and his angels' — primary designation for non-human entities - Ezek 28:18-19 — Satan-figure (anointed cherub) becomes 'ashes upon the earth' and exists 'never any more' - Rev 2:10 — Paired with 2:11 — 'be thou faithful unto death' (first death) precedes second death exemption - Dan 7:11 — Fourth beast 'slain, body destroyed, given to the burning flame' — OT destruction language for beast
