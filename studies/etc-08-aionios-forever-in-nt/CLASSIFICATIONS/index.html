    CLASSIFICATIONS.md — Audit Trail for etc7-08-aionios-forever-in-nt¶

    This file contains the full Decision Tree walkthrough for every E-item classified Cond. or ECT and every N-item. Items classified Neutral at E-tier are not walked through here (they passed V1=NO and V2=NO, producing Neutral by default).

    E-Item Classifications¶

    E1 (Rom 16:25) -- "The mystery was kept secret in aionios times -- a period that has ended" -- Classified: Cond.¶

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- The passage does not directly use destruction/cessation vocabulary applied to the wicked. V2 -- No conscious-torment vocabulary. However, this item is not about the fate of the wicked directly; it is about aionios referring to a period that ended. Both V1 and V2 = NO.
    • Reclassification: This item describes aionios referring to past time that has ended. It does not use destruction or torment vocabulary. Neither V1 nor V2 applies.
    • Result: Classification revised to Neutral. The observation that aionios refers to past time is a factual textual observation both sides acknowledge.

    Final: E1 reclassified to Neutral.

    E2 (2 Tim 1:9) -- "God's grace given before aionios times -- aionios refers to past time" -- Classified: Cond.¶

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 = NO (no destruction vocabulary). V2 = NO (no torment vocabulary).
    • Result: Neither vocabulary applies. This is a factual observation about aionios referring to past time.
    • Result: Classification revised to Neutral.

    Final: E2 reclassified to Neutral.

    E3 (Tit 1:2) -- "Aionios appears twice in one verse: for eternal life (future) and before aionios times (past)" -- Classified: Cond.¶

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 = NO. V2 = NO.
    • Result: This is a grammatical observation about the same adjective having different temporal scopes. Neither destruction nor torment vocabulary.
    • Result: Classification revised to Neutral.

    Final: E3 reclassified to Neutral.

    E4 (Jude 1:6-7) -- "Jude uses aidios for angelic chains (v.6) but aionios for Sodom's fire (v.7) in adjacent verses" -- Classified: Cond.¶

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 = NO directly (the observation is about word choice, not about destruction vocabulary applied to wicked). V2 = NO.
    • Result: This is a textual observation about an author's word selection. Both ECT and conditionalist scholars can verify Jude uses different words. Neither destruction nor torment vocabulary is the focus.
    • Result: Classification revised to Neutral.

    Final: E4 reclassified to Neutral.

    E5 (2 Thess 1:9) -- "The wicked shall be punished with everlasting destruction (olethron aionion)" -- Classified: Cond.¶

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- "destruction" (olethros) applied to the wicked. YES. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): The grammatical subject is "them that know not God, and that obey not the gospel" (2 Thess 1:8) -- literal human beings. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): Greek parsing from raw data: olethron (G3639, Acc Sg M) aionion (G166, Acc Sg M). Olethros = "destruction/ruin." The grammar unambiguously supports the reading: the penalty is destruction, modified by aionios. The phrase "apo prosopou tou kyriou" ("from the presence of the Lord") is a prepositional phrase; its attachment is debated (source of destruction vs. separation from), but the core reading -- the penalty is destruction -- is grammatically unambiguous. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Pauline epistle, didactic teaching about eschatological judgment. Didactic prose. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with E-items using destruction vocabulary: Rom 6:23 (wages = death), Phil 3:19 (end = destruction), Matt 10:28 (destroy soul and body). No conflicting E-item states the wicked receive something other than destruction from Paul. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    Final: E5 = Cond.

    E6 (Matt 25:46a) -- "These shall go away into everlasting punishment (kolasin aionion)" -- Classified: Neutral¶

    This item states what the text says: the wicked go into kolasin aionion. Both sides accept this is what the verse says. The positional question (whether kolasis means ongoing torment or permanent punitive result) is an interpretive question, making any positional classification an inference.

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- "punishment" (kolasis) is a penalty word, not strictly destruction vocabulary. V2 -- kolasis is not specifically conscious-torment vocabulary (the torment word is basanizo). Neither keyword list is a clean match.
    • Both V1 and V2 = NO in strict terms. Kolasis is a penalty word that could describe either a destructive penalty or an ongoing penalty depending on interpretation.
    • Result: Neutral. The text says what it says; what kolasis means in this context is the subject of inference.

    Final: E6 = Neutral.

    E7 (John 3:16) -- "Whosoever believeth should not perish (apollytai) but have everlasting life" -- Classified: Cond.¶

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- "perish" (apollymi) applied to those who do not believe. YES. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): "Whosoever" -- generic humanity. Literal human beings. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): apollytai (G622, Aor Mid Subj 3S) = "might perish." Grammar is unambiguous: the alternative to having eternal life is perishing. No alternative parsing available. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Jesus's direct speech in John's Gospel, didactic teaching to Nicodemus. Didactic. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with Rom 6:23 (death vs. life), Matt 7:13 (destruction vs. life), Gal 6:8 (corruption vs. life). No conflicting E-item. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    Final: E7 = Cond.

    E8 (Rom 6:23) -- "The wages of sin is death; the gift of God is eternal life" -- Classified: Cond.¶

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- "death" (thanatos) as the wages of sin. YES. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): Generic statement about sin and its consequence. Applies to literal human beings. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): thanatos (G2288, Nom Sg M) = "death." zoe aionios (G2222 Nom Sg F + G166 Nom Sg F) = "eternal life." Charisma (G5486, "gift") vs. opsonia (G3800, "wages"). Grammar is unambiguous. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Pauline epistle, didactic theological statement. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with John 3:16, Gal 6:8, Matt 7:13-14. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    Final: E8 = Cond.

    E9 (Jude 1:7 / 2 Pet 2:6) -- "Sodom suffered eternal fire (pyros aioniou) yet became ashes" -- Classified: Cond.¶

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- 2 Pet 2:6 says "turning the cities of Sodom and Gomorrha into ashes." Ashes = destruction/cessation vocabulary applied to the subject of "eternal fire." YES. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): The cities of Sodom and Gomorrah and their inhabitants. Historical human beings and their cities. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): pyros (G4442, Gen Sg N) aioniou (G166, Gen Sg N) = "of eternal fire." Tephrosas (2 Pet 2:6) = "having turned to ashes" (aorist active participle). Grammar unambiguously supports: the eternal fire produced ashes. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Jude's epistle (didactic) and 2 Peter (didactic). Both are epistolary teaching. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with the fire-brimstone paradigm (Gen 19:24-28). Consistent with the observation that "eternal fire" does not require endless burning. No conflicting E-item. PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    Final: E9 = Cond.

    E10 (Matt 10:28) -- "God can destroy both soul and body in gehenna" -- Classified: Cond.¶

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- "destroy" (apollymi) applied to soul and body in gehenna. YES. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): "Soul and body" of human beings. PASS.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): apolesai (G622, Aor Act Inf) = "to destroy." Both psyche (soul) and soma (body) are direct objects. Grammar unambiguous. PASS.
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Jesus's direct teaching in the Synoptic Gospels, didactic instruction to disciples. PASS.
    • Gate 4 (Harmony): Consistent with John 3:16 (perish), Rom 6:23 (death). PASS.
    • Result: All gates pass. Classification: Cond. stands.

    Final: E10 = Cond.

    E11 (Rev 20:9) -- "Fire from God devoured them" -- Classified: Neutral¶

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V1 -- "devoured" (katephagen) = destruction vocabulary. YES. Candidate: COND.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): "Gog and Magog" armies. In Revelation's apocalyptic framework, these are prophetically typological. FAIL -- subject is a prophetic type/figure (nations used as prophetic type).
    • Step 3 Reclassification:
    • RC1: Subject is prophetic nations in an apocalyptic vision (Rev 20:8-9).
    • RC2: Corrected observation: "In an apocalyptic vision, prophetic nations are devoured by fire from God."
    • RC3: Re-enter V1/V2: destruction vocabulary in an apocalyptic vision does not constitute didactic teaching about individual human fate. Neither V1 nor V2 applies to the corrected observation.
    • Result: Classification revised to Neutral.

    Final: E11 reclassified to Neutral.

    E12 (Rev 20:10) -- "Devil, beast, false prophet tormented for ever and ever" -- Classified: Neutral¶

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V2 -- "tormented" (basanisthesontai) = conscious-torment vocabulary. YES. Candidate: ECT.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): The devil (non-human supernatural being), the beast (symbolic entity, Rev 17:8), the false prophet (symbolic entity = second beast of Rev 13:11-17). FAIL -- none of the three is a literal human being.
    • Step 3 Reclassification:
    • RC1: All three subjects are non-human/symbolic entities.
    • RC2: Corrected observation: "Non-human and symbolic entities are depicted as tormented in an apocalyptic vision."
    • RC3: Re-enter V1/V2: torment of non-human/symbolic entities in a vision does not constitute evidence about literal human fate. Neither applies.
    • Result: Classification: Neutral.

    Final: E12 = Neutral.

    E13 (Rev 14:11) -- "Smoke of their torment ascends for ever and ever" -- Classified: Neutral¶

    • Step 1 Vocabulary Scan: V2 -- "torment" (basanismos) = conscious-torment vocabulary. YES. Candidate: ECT.
    • Step 2 Validation Gates:
    • Gate 1 (Subject): "Those who worship the beast and his image" -- described within Revelation's symbolic framework. These are human worshipers, but identified by their relationship to symbolic entities.
    • Gate 2 (Grammar): The SMOKE ascends (kapnos anabainei), not the persons. The grammar describes ascending smoke -- evidence of completed burning (cf. Gen 19:28).
    • Gate 3 (Genre): Apocalyptic vision within Revelation. FAIL -- apocalyptic genre.
