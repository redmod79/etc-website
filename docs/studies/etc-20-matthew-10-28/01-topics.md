### PUNISHMENT

**DEATH PENALTY**
- Will not be commuted NUM 35:31
- In the Mosaic law the death penalty was inflicted for:
  - Murder GEN 9:5,6; NUM 35:16-21,30-33; DEU 17:6
  - Adultery LEV 20:10; DEU 22:24
  - Incest LEV 20:11,12,14
  - Bestiality EXO 22:19; LEV 20:15,16
  - Sodomy LEV 18:22; 20:13
  - Sexual immorality DEU 22:21-24
  - Rape of a betrothed virgin DEU 22:25
  - Perjury ZEC 5:4
  - Kidnapping EXO 21:16; DEU 24:7
  - Promiscuousness of a priest's daughter LEV 21:9
  - Witchcraft EXO 22:18
  - Offering human sacrifice LEV 20:2-5
  - Striking or cursing parents EXO 21:15,17; LEV 20:9
  - Disobedience to parents DEU 21:18-21
  - Stealing ZEC 5:3,4
  - Blasphemy LEV 24:11-14,16,23
  - Desecrating the Sabbath EXO 35:2; NUM 15:32-36
  - False prophecy DEU 13:1-10
  - Sacrificing to false gods EXO 22:20
  - Refusing to abide by court decisions DEU 17:12
  - Treason 1KI 2:25; EST 2:23
  - Sedition ACT 5:36,37
