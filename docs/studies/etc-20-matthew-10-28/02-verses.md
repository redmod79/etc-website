**Key Synoptic Differences:**
- Matthew 10:28 says "destroy [apollymi] both soul and body in hell [gehenna]"
- Luke 12:5 says "after he hath killed [apokteino] hath power to cast into hell [gehenna]"
- Matthew uses [apollymi](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g622/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (destroy); Luke uses [apokteino](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g615/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} + emballo (kill + cast into)
- Matthew mentions [psyche](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g5590/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (soul); Luke does not mention the soul at all
- Luke specifies "after he hath killed" -- a temporal sequence (kill first, then cast)
