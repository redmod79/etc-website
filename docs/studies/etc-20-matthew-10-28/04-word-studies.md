### Key Verses Where [G5590](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g5590/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} = "life"
Mat 2:20; 6:25 (2x); 10:39 (2x); 16:25 (2x); 20:28; Mar 3:4; 8:35 (2x); 10:45; Luk 6:9; 9:24 (2x); 12:22,23; 14:26; 17:33; Act 20:10,24; 27:22; Rom 11:3; 16:4; Rev 8:9

### Same-Author (Matthew) Usage Pattern
Matthew uses [psyche](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g5590/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} approximately 16 times (counting each occurrence in multi-use verses):
- Translated "life" (8x): 2:20; 6:25 (2x); 10:39 (2x); 16:25 (2x); 20:28
- Translated "soul(s)" (8x): 10:28 (2x); 11:29; 12:18; 16:26 (2x); 22:37; 26:38

**Critical observation:** Matthew uses [psyche](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g5590/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} for "life" and "soul" in nearly equal proportion. In every "life" usage, the context is physical life that can be lost, sought, saved, or given. In 10:28, the same word is used twice -- once for what men cannot kill and once for what God can destroy. The immediate context (10:39, just 11 verses later) uses the exact same word to mean "life" in the lose/find saying. This same-author proximity is a key datum.

**Critical finding:** The LXX translates [H6](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h6/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (abad) as [G622](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g622/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (apollymi) 141 times -- the dominant Hebrew-Greek word pair for destruction. The [abad](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h6/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"}-[apollymi](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g622/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} lexical chain is the primary vocabulary for the fate of the wicked across both testaments. No lexicon defines [apollymi](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g622/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} as "torment."

### Key Verses by Translation
**"destroy":** Mat 21:41; 27:20; Mar 9:22; 12:9; Luk 20:16
**"perish":** Mat 9:17; Luk 13:3,5,33; 15:17; 21:18; Rom 2:12; 1Co 8:11; 2Co 2:15; 2Th 2:10
**"lost":** Mat 10:6; 15:24; Luk 15:24,32

### Same-Author (Matthew) Usage Pattern
Matthew uses [apollymi](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g622/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} approximately 18 times across 15 verses:

**Destruction/killing (active, transitive):**
- 2:13 -- Herod seeks to "destroy" the child (physical killing)
- 10:28 -- God is able to "destroy" both soul and body in [gehenna](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1067/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}
- 12:14 -- Pharisees plot to "destroy" Jesus (physical killing)
- 21:41 -- The lord will "destroy" those wicked men (judgment)
- 22:7 -- The king "destroyed" those murderers (judgment)
- 27:20 -- The crowd wants to "destroy" Jesus (physical killing)

**Perishing (intransitive, middle voice):**
- 5:29,30 -- one member should "perish" (better than whole body in [gehenna](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1067/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"})
- 8:25 -- "we perish" (physical death in storm)
- 9:17 -- bottles "perish" (physical ruin)
- 18:14 -- "one of these little ones should perish"
- 26:52 -- "shall perish with the sword" (physical death)

**Lost/ruined (participle, passive):**
- 10:6; 15:24 -- "lost sheep" (spiritually ruined, headed for destruction)
- 18:11 -- "save that which was lost"

**Lose (transitive, forfeiture):**
- 10:39 (2x); 16:25 (2x) -- "lose his life/[psyche](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g5590/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}"
- 10:42 -- "lose his reward"

**Key patterns in Matthew:**
1. When God or authority figures "destroy" the wicked (10:28; 21:41; 22:7), the meaning is actual destruction/death, not torment.
2. The "lost" sheep usage (10:6; 15:24; 18:11) describes those headed toward perishing whom Jesus came to save.
3. In the same chapter (10:28 and 10:39), [apollymi](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g622/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} is used for both divine destruction and the lose/find life paradox.

## 3. [G1067](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1067/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}: [gehenna](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1067/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} -- "Valley of [Hinnom](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h2011/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"}; hell"

- **Transliteration:** [geenna](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1067/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}

### Same-Author (Matthew) Usage Pattern
Matthew contains 7 of 12 total NT [gehenna](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1067/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} occurrences (58%). Key observations:

1. **Mat 5:22** -- [gehenna](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1067/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} paired with escalating judgment (judgment > council > hell fire)
2. **Mat 5:29-30** -- [gehenna](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1067/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} paired with [apollymi](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g622/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (perish): "one member should perish... whole body cast into hell"
3. **Mat 10:28** -- [gehenna](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1067/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} paired with [apollymi](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g622/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (destroy): "destroy both soul and body in hell"
4. **Mat 18:9** -- [gehenna](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1067/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} paired with "enter into life" contrast: life vs. hell fire
5. **Mat 23:15** -- [gehenna](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1067/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} used metaphorically: "child of hell" (character description)
6. **Mat 23:33** -- [gehenna](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1067/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} paired with [krisis](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2920/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (judgment): "damnation of hell"

**In every Matthean [gehenna](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1067/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} passage, the associated vocabulary is destruction/loss/casting/judgment, never [basanizo](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g928/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (torment).** The word [basanizo](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g928/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} does not appear in any [gehenna](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1067/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} verse in the entire NT.

### Role in Matt 10:28
In Matthew 10:28, [soma](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4983/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} appears twice:
1. "kill the **body** [soma]" -- what men can do

The verse sets up a body-soul pair. Men can kill the body but not the soul. God can destroy both. The question is whether "destroy both soul and body" means destroy the entire person (body + life/self), or whether it implies a separable soul entity that survives bodily death.

## 6. [G615](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g615/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}: [apokteino](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g615/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} -- "to kill outright"

### Lexical Data
- **Greek:** [ἀποκτείνω](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g615/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}
- **Transliteration:** [apokteino](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g615/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}
- **Pronunciation:** ap-ok-ti-no
- **Part of Speech:** verb (v)
- **BLB Count:** 75 occurrences
- **KJV Translation DB Count:** 67

### Definition (Strong's)
From [G575](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g575/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (apo) and [G2889](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2889/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (kteino, to slay); to kill outright; figuratively, to destroy.

### Translation Distribution
| Translation | Count | % |

| killed | 7 | 10.4% |
| be killed | 5 | 7.5% |

### Key Verses
**[G615](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g615/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} = "kill":** Mat 10:28; 26:4; Luk 12:4; 13:31; Act 27:42; Rev 9:5; 11:7

### Role in Matt 10:28 and Luke 12:4-5
In **Matthew 10:28**, [apokteino](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g615/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (kill) is used for what men do to the body, while [apollymi](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g622/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (destroy) is used for what God does to soul and body. This word change is significant: men can "kill outright" ([apokteino](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g615/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}) the body, but God can "destroy fully" ([apollymi](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g622/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}) both soul and body. The different verbs suggest different scopes of action.

In **Luke 12:4-5**, [apokteino](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g615/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} is used in both clauses:
- v.4: men "kill" ([apokteino](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g615/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}) the body
- v.5: God "after he hath killed" ([apokteino](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g615/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}) has power to cast into [gehenna](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1067/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}

Luke uses [apokteino](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g615/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} for both human and divine action, while Matthew uses [apokteino](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g615/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} for human action and switches to [apollymi](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g622/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} for divine action. This switch is a key datum for understanding what Matthew means by "destroy."

**Key grammatical observations:**
1. Both "kill" ([apokteino](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g615/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}) and "destroy" ([apollymi](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g622/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}) are **aorist active infinitives** -- punctiliar action, not ongoing.
2. The subject who "destroys" is described as "the one being able" (ton dynamenon) -- a present participle indicating ongoing capability.
3. "In [gehenna](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1067/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}" (en geenne) is dative of place/sphere.
4. The construction "kai psychen kai [soma](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4983/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}" (both soul and body) uses the correlative kai...kai construction, emphasizing the totality: the entire person.
