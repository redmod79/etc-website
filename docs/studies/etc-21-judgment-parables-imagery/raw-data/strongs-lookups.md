  - "burn" = Matt 13:30; Rev 17:16
  - "was burnt up" = Rev 8:7 (2x)
  - "shall be burned up" = 2 Pet 3:10
  - "she shall be utterly burned" = Rev 18:8

## [G2575](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2575/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} kaminos -- "furnace"
- 4 total occurrences, 2 unique translations
- furnace (3), a furnace (1)
- Lexicon: "probably from kaio; a furnace"
- Biblical Usage: Gen 19:28; Exo 19:18; Jer 11:4; Dan 3:6; Matt 13:42; 13:50; Rev 1:15; 9:2
- Verse lookups:
  - "furnace" = Matt 13:42; 13:50; Rev 9:2
  - "a furnace" = Rev 1:15
- Hebrew sources: [H861](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h861/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} attuwn (Aramaic furnace, Dan 3), [H3564](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h3564/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} kuwr (furnace, Deut 4:20 etc.), [H3345](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h3345/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} yeqad (burning), [H5135](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h5135/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} nuwr (fire, Aramaic)
- Note: In LXX, kaminos translates [H3564](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h3564/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} kuwr 6 times (PMI 9.49). [H8574](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h8574/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} tannuwr is mapped to klibanos (G2823) not kaminos.

## [H8574](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h8574/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} tannuwr -- "fire-pot, oven, furnace"
- 19 occurrences, 12 translations
- oven (3), furnace (2), in the oven (2), of the furnaces (2), like an oven (2), as an oven (2)...
- Lexicon: "from nahar; a fire-pot"
- Biblical Usage: Gen 15:17; Lev 2:4; 7:9; 11:35; 26:26; Neh 3:11; 12:38; Psa 21:10; Isa 31:9; Lam 5:10; Hos 7:4,6,7; Mal 4:1
- LXX mapping: Maps primarily to klibanos (G2823, count 12, PMI 10.56) -- NOT kaminos (G2575)
- Note: Mal 4:1 uses tannuwr, translated "oven" in KJV ("burn as an oven"). LXX uses klibanos for this, not kaminos. This means Matt 13:42 (kaminos) is NOT directly quoting Mal 4:1's Hebrew, but may be drawing on the broader furnace imagery.

## [H3564](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h3564/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} kuwr -- "pot, furnace"
- 11 occurrences
- of the furnace (4), furnace (2), and the furnace (2), out of (1), in the furnace (1), from (1)
- Lexicon: "a pot or furnace (as if excavated)"
- Biblical Usage: Deut 4:20; 1 Ki 8:51; Pro 17:3; 27:21; Isa 48:10; Jer 11:4; Ezk 22:18,20,22
- LXX mapping: Maps to kaminos (G2575, count 6, PMI 9.49) -- THIS is the Hebrew furnace word behind kaminos
- Note: kuwr is used for the "iron furnace" of Egypt (Deut 4:20; 1 Ki 8:51; Jer 11:4) and the refining/melting furnace (Pro 17:3; Ezk 22:18-22). The LXX translated kuwr as kaminos, making this the primary OT background for the NT kaminos.
