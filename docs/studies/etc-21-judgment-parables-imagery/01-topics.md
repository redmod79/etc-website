# Topics Research

## Question
What do Jesus' judgment parables and associated imagery teach about the fate of the wicked? Focus on: Matt 13:24-30,36-43 (wheat & tares / furnace of fire), Matt 13:47-50 (dragnet), 'outer darkness' (Matt 8:12; 22:13; 25:30), 'weeping and gnashing of teeth' (7 occurrences), and the fire/furnace vocabulary.

### FURNACE
**-Use of**
- For refining silver: EZK 22:22; MAL 3:3
- For refining gold: PRO 17:3
- For melting lead and tin: EZK 22:20

**-For capital punishment:** Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego cast into, by Nebuchadnezzar: DAN 3:6-26

**-FIGURATIVE**
- Of affliction: DEU 4:20; 1KI 8:51; PSA 12:6; ISA 48:10; JER 11:4
- Of lust: HOS 7:4
- **Of hell:** MAL 4:1; MAT 13:42,50; REV 9:2

Note: The figurative "of hell" references are the core texts for this study. Mal 4:1 uses "oven/furnace" (H8574 tannuwr) and says the wicked "shall be stubble" that the day "shall burn them up...it shall leave them neither root nor branch." Matt 13:42,50 use kaminos (G2575) in the phrase "furnace of fire." Rev 9:2 uses kaminos as a simile for the smoke from the bottomless pit.

## Related Topics Discovered

### Nave's Categorization Observations
1. **FURNACE "of hell"** -- Nave's categorizes Matt 13:42,50 under FURNACE, figurative of hell, alongside Mal 4:1 and Rev 9:2.
2. **FIRE "of the destruction of the wicked"** -- Nave's categorizes the same Matt 13:42,50 under FIRE as destruction imagery, using the heading "destruction of the wicked."
3. **TORMENTS** -- Only lists Luke 16:23-28 and Rev 14:10,11. The judgment parables (Matt 13, Matt 22, Matt 25) are NOT listed under TORMENTS.
4. **JUDGMENT "the general"** -- Lists the full sweep of judgment passages including all our study texts.
5. **DARKNESS "outer darkness"** -- Specifically catalogued as a sub-category under DARKNESS, figurative of judgments.

This categorization pattern is significant: Nave's places the judgment parables under JUDGMENT and FIRE/FURNACE (destruction language), not under TORMENTS.
