1. **The Genitive of Time Construction (hemeras kai nuktos):**
READ `$GRAMMAR_REF/greek/genitive-of-time.md` — this entry specifically covers the hemeras kai nuktos pattern including all 5 Revelation occurrences. The genitive of time expresses 'time within which' or 'during which,' characterizing the temporal backdrop of an action rather than measuring its extent. The paired merism hemeras kai nuktos is a special subtype expressing continuous uninterrupted activity spanning the full day-night cycle. The critical diagnostic contrast is Luke 2:37, which uses the ACCUSATIVE nykta kai hemeran (measuring extent of Anna's service) vs. the GENITIVE in Rev 4:8 etc. (characterizing the activity as filling the day-night framework). Run greek_parser.py --verse on Rev 4:8, Rev 7:15, Rev 12:10, Rev 14:11, Rev 20:10, Luke 2:37, and Matt 24:20 to verify case forms. Also run semantic_grammar.py 'genitive of time' --greek for Wallace/BDF textbook citations beyond what the library entry provides.
**Verses:** Rev 4:8, Rev 7:15, Rev 12:10, Rev 14:11, Rev 20:10, Luke 2:37, Matt 24:20
**Strong's:** G2250, G3571

7. **The Anapausis Antithesis: Rev 14:11 vs. Rev 14:13:**
READ `$WORD_STUDIES/G372-anapausis.md` and `$WORD_STUDIES/G373-anapauo.md`. Rev 14:11 uses the noun anapausis (G372, rest/intermission) — the beast-worshippers 'have no rest (anapausin) day nor night.' Rev 14:13 uses the cognate verb anapauo (G373, to rest/cease) — the blessed dead 'rest (anapauontai or anapausontai) from their labours.' Same root, two verses apart, opposite outcomes. The beast-worshippers receive NO anapausis; the faithful dead receive FULL anapauo. This is a deliberate literary structure — John places the antithesis in adjacent verses to define 'no rest' by contrast with 'rest.' If the faithful's 'rest' is the cessation of labor and the beginning of reward, then the beast-worshippers' 'no rest' is the absence of relief during their judgment. Run greek_parser.py --verse on both Rev 14:11 and Rev 14:13.
**Verses:** Rev 14:11, Rev 14:13, Matt 11:28-29, Heb 4:1-11
**Strong's:** G372, G373
