### Semantic Range
The noun [aion](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g165/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} has a broader semantic range than most English translations suggest:
- **"Age" (temporal period):** Matt 12:32; 13:22,39,40,49; 24:3; 28:20; Lk 20:34-35; Rom 12:2; 1 Cor 1:20; 2:6-8; 2 Cor 4:4; Eph 2:2,7; 3:9
- **"World" (as temporal location):** Heb 1:2; 9:26; 11:3 -- the created temporal order
- **"Ever/evermore" (duration formula):** Most doxological passages, the "ages of ages" construction
- **"Ages" (plural):** Eph 2:7; 3:9 -- multiple ages, distinct periods
- **Key insight:** When used in the formula "this age" vs "the age to come," [aion](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g165/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} clearly means a bounded temporal period, and this is the root word from which [aionios](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g166/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} is formed

## [G126](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g126/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}: aidios (adjective)

### Lexical Data
- **Greek:** aidios
- **Transliteration:** aidios
- **Pronunciation:** ah-id-ee-os
- **Part of speech:** adjective
- **BLB Count:** 2 occurrences
- **Definition:** From [G104](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g104/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (aei, "always"); everduring (forward and backward, or forward only)
- **Derivation:** From aei (G104, "always/ever") -- NOT from [aion](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g165/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (G165)

### KJV Translation Distribution
| Translation | Count | % |
|---|---|---|
| eternal | 1 | 50% |
| in everlasting | 1 | 50% |

### Occurrences
1. **Romans 1:20** -- "his **eternal** power and Godhead" (applied to God)
2. **Jude 1:6** -- "**everlasting** chains under darkness" (applied to angelic imprisonment)

### No Hebrew Source Found
The LXX mapping tool found no Hebrew source words for aidios. This is a distinctly Greek word with no direct Hebrew equivalent, derived from the Greek concept of "always" (aei), not from the Hebrew concept of [olam](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h5769/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"}.

### Comparison: Aidios vs. [Aionios](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g166/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}
| Feature | Aidios (G126) | [Aionios](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g166/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (G166) |
|---|---|---|
| Root | aei (G104, "always") | [aion](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g165/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (G165, "age") |
| Inherent meaning | "always-existing, everduring" | "pertaining to an age" |
| Occurrences | 2 | 71 |
| Applied to God | Yes (Rom 1:20) | Yes (Rom 16:26, etc.) |
| Applied to life | No | Yes (42+ times) |
| Applied to punishment | No directly (chains, not the punishment itself) | Yes (7 times) |
| Applied to past time | No | Yes (3 times) |
| Hebrew source | None (purely Greek) | [H5769](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h5769/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} [olam](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h5769/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (primarily) |

**Critical observation:** If Greek had only one word for "eternal/endless," we would expect that word to be used consistently. The existence of aidios (inherently meaning "ever-existing") alongside [aionios](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g166/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (meaning "age-related") suggests the NT authors had a choice and deliberately chose [aionios](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g166/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (the age-related word) for most purposes, using aidios only twice.

## [H5769](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h5769/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"}: [olam](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h5769/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (Hebrew -- background for aionios)

### Relevance
The primary Hebrew word that both [aion](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g165/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (G165) and [aionios](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g166/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (G166) translate in the LXX. The semantic range of [olam](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h5769/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"} directly informs how [aionios](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g166/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} would have been understood by readers steeped in the Hebrew scriptures.

### Key OT Examples of Limited Duration
1. **Exodus 21:6** -- A slave serves "le-[olam](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h5769/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"}" (forever) = until death
2. **Jonah 2:6** -- "The earth with her bars was about me le-[olam](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h5769/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"}" = three days
3. **1 Samuel 2:30** -- God said the priestly house would serve "le-[olam](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h5769/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"}" but then revoked it
4. **Jer 18:16** -- Desolation "le-[olam](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h5769/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"}" of cities that were later rebuilt
5. **Hab 3:6** -- The "[olam](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h5769/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"}" mountains were scattered -- they are temporal

### OT Examples of Truly Endless Duration
1. **Psa 90:2** -- "From everlasting to everlasting, thou art God"
2. **Dan 12:2** -- "Everlasting life... everlasting contempt"
3. **Gen 3:22** -- "Live le-[olam](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h5769/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"}" with tree of life access

### Conclusion on [Olam](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h5769/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"}'s Semantic Range
[Olam](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h5769/kjv/wlc/0-1/){:target="_blank"}'s duration is determined by the nature of the subject, not by the word itself. When applied to God (who is by nature endless), it means truly endless. When applied to a slave's service, it means a lifetime. This inherited semantic flexibility is critical for understanding [aionios](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g166/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}.

Occurs 42+ times in this pairing, making "[aionios](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g166/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} life" the most common use of [aionios](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g166/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} by far.

### [G2851](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2851/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}: [kolasis](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2851/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (punishment/pruning)
Used in Matt 25:46 -- "[kolasis](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2851/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} [aionios](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g166/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}." [Kolasis](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2851/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} originally meant "pruning" or "corrective punishment" (vs. timoria, "retributive punishment"). Some scholars argue this term itself suggests remedial rather than purely retributive punishment.

### [G3639](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g3639/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}: [olethros](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g3639/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} (destruction/ruin)
Used in 2 Thess 1:9 -- "[olethros](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g3639/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} [aionios](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g166/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}." The word means ruin, destruction. Combined with [aionios](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g166/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}, the question is whether this means "destruction that is age-lasting" or "destruction that is eternal."

### [G4442](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4442/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"}: pur (fire)
Used with [aionios](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g166/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} in Matt 18:8; 25:41; Jude 1:7. The "[aionios](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g166/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} fire" of Jude 1:7 is applied to Sodom, whose fire demonstrably went out, raising the question of whether [aionios](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g166/kjv/tr/0-1/){:target="_blank"} describes the fire's duration or its source/quality.
