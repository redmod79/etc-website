How does the Hebrew mind understand smoke ascending up forever and ever? What does the smoke represent in the biblical worldview? This study focuses specifically on SMOKE IMAGERY — why smoke, what did it signify, how does it function across the OT and NT? ## Prior Research Summary
Eleven prior studies and four word studies provide an established foundation. The olam/aionios semantic range is settled (subject-determined duration). The Rev 14:11 formula traces to Isa 34:10. The smoke-ascending formula means permanent memorial of completed destruction. Basanismos is genre-restricted to Revelation. Subject differentiation at the lake of fire is established. The ashan (H6227) word study identifies five semantic categories (theophanic, judgment, transience, military, eschatological). The kapnos (G2586) study shows 13 NT occurrences concentrated in Revelation. The TR-ashan-kapnos study confirms no semantic shift across the LXX bridge. The WG-smoke study maps the four-word apocalyptic judgment cluster (pyr + kapnos + theion + pyrosis). What remains unstudied: the UNIFIED Hebrew conceptual framework for smoke across all categories, WHY smoke was chosen as the image, the smoke-as-witness vs fire-as-agent distinction, the theophanic-to-judgment smoke continuity, smoke as testimony/communication, the incense-judgment smoke symmetry, and the qitor/ashan lexical distinction.
